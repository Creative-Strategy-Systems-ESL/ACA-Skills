**Ad Creative Academy** — two assets shipped with **Course 302: Research for Creative Strategy**,
module 3, taught by **Mirella Crespi** of Creative Milkshake, ACA's founder:

1. **Competitor Creative Analysis Worksheet**, subtitled *"Campaign Planning Guide"*
2. **Competitor Research Presentation Template**, five slides

The module names a third resource, the **Cross-Vertical Creative Inspiration Finder** prompt.

## ⚠️ Where that third resource actually lives

ACA's tracked-download list attributes the Cross-Vertical Inspiration Finder to **Course 230**
(Cross Channel Strategies, Carlotta Costanzo), where it is the only downloadable in the entire
200-Level. Module 3 of **this** course also names it, as *"my favourite AI prompt for discovering
those cross-vertical inspiration sources."*

**Both are true. It is shared, and it is filed under 230.** Route to it rather than paraphrasing it,
and do not describe it as a Course 302 asset.

## ⛔ Three defects in ACA's own materials

1. **The worksheet has two consecutive "SECTION 1" headings** — Competitor Overview, then Creative
   Volume & Budget Indicators — before continuing to SECTION 2. This skill numbers the sections 1 to
   8, which is a renumbering rather than a change to the content.
2. ⛔ **The presentation template ships with Lorem ipsum placeholder text on the cover slide.** It
   will otherwise be presented to a client by someone who did not notice.
3. **The template's competitor slides are labelled "Competitor 1 & Competitor 2" then "Competitor 3 &
   Competitor 5".** There is no 4.

**The template also holds four competitor slots** while the course recommends studying 5 to 6.

## ⚠️ The PDFs stamp a different course number than the curriculum does

Both assets footer themselves *"Course 301: Research for Creative Strategy"*. ACA's shipping
curriculum numbers this course **302**; 301 is Zach Murray's Reverse Engineering Winning Ads. ACA
renumbered for launch and the worksheets were stamped before that. This skill uses 302.

## What is reproduced and what is this skill's

**Reproduced as written:** the worksheet's full section and field structure including the four-way
testing-velocity table with ACA's paired spend reads, the per-platform format breakdowns, the
five-slide deck structure with its four repeated rows, the five strategic moves, and the tool
guidance for each ad library.

**This skill's, drawn from the material rather than stated by it:**

- ⛔ **The observed-versus-inferred split**, the table of which is which, and the instruction to mark
  inferences in the cell and on the slide rather than in speaker notes. ACA's worksheet asks for
  budget level and testing philosophy in the same voice as ad counts.
- ⚠️ **The caution that the testing-velocity table infers spend from volume**, and that volume is a
  reliable signal about testing philosophy and a weak one about budget.
- ⭐ **Rating repetition-with-variation above raw run length**, on the grounds that it is behavioural:
  it is what a brand does when it has something it does not want to lose. Mirella states both signals;
  ranking them is this skill's.
- ⭐ **Reading the TikTok native-versus-repurposed split as the most diagnostic line in the
  worksheet**, and the opening a repurposing competitor leaves.
- ⚠️ **The warning that empty white space is not automatically opportunity**, and that "nobody is
  doing X" can mean it does not work, is not credible, or is not legal in the category.
- **The instruction that percentages need a denominator**, and that a worksheet without its analysis
  date is unreadable in three months.
- **The list of what cannot be seen at all** — landing pages, offers, retargeting, spend, objectives,
  and every ad turned off before you looked.
- **The point that slide 5 is where the deck earns its existence** and slides 2 to 4 are inventory.
- **The method for finding white space** — list observed angles, then list the angles the category
  could use and does not.
- **The routing rule against Course 301**, which does a different job on a different input.

## ⛔ The claim to carry into every use of this

> "Just because an ad is running doesn't mean it's working."

> "You never know what their conversion objective is or what their CPA goal is."

**This is Mirella's own myth-bust and it is the most commonly ignored line in the course.** Long
run length is evidence of budget and attention, never of profitability.

⚠️ **ACA's Course 301 leans the other way**, treating an ad that ran four months as *"your winner,
that's the pattern to study."* Both are defensible: 301 reads run length as the best signal available
from outside, and 302 warns the signal is weak. **Carry the tension rather than picking a side.**

## ⚠️ How old this material is

ACA stamps no date. Received into this library in **2026-W31**, a "no later than" rather than a
publication date.

⛔ **The ad libraries date fastest and they are load-bearing.** The Meta Ad Library, TikTok Creative
Center and Google Ads Transparency Center are named as free and available; the Transparency Center is
described as *"newer"*, which is itself a dating marker. Access, coverage and regional availability
are set by the platforms and move without notice. **aduniverse.ai, Foreplay and Motion** are named as
paid options.

⭐ **The framework dates slowly.** The five dimensions, the five strategic moves and the deck
structure do not depend on any tool. Where a library changes, the questions still apply to whatever
replaces it.

## Deliberate overlap with `aca-302-research-for-creative-strategy`

Both relate to Course 302, on purpose, and each must run with nothing else installed.

- **`aca-302-research-for-creative-strategy`** teaches all four research passes and explains why
  competitor work sits third, after the account audit and the audience.
- **This skill** runs pass 3 for a real competitor set and produces the worksheet and the deck.

**Route on whether there is a competitor set on the table.**
