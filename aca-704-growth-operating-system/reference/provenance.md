**One recording backs it**, a delivered session with timecodes. ⚠️ **There is no script counterpart**,
so nothing here can be checked against a second reading of the same material.

⭐ **Reclassified in 2026-W33.** This library held it as a *standalone masterclass outside the
curriculum numbering*. ACA re-sent it under their own filename, *704 - Mirella Growth - Growth

**ACA's tracked-download list names two assets for this course and both are held**: the Audience

## ⛔ The algorithm claims are a practitioner's reading

**This is the most important caution in this skill.** The course's foundation is an account of how
Meta's Andromeda update works, and Mirella flags it herself: *"here's what we know so far."*

| Claim | Status |
|---|---|
| The old system ignored the order and timing of events | Her characterisation |
| The new system uses **sequence learning** on timestamped event streams | Her characterisation of publicly discussed Meta work |
| Meta built a **hierarchical ad index** across format, messaging, persona, visual style and hook structure | Her characterisation |
| ⛔ **The "grouping penalty"** — near-identical ads get grouped and return less | ⛔ **Her term.** An observed effect, not a documented Meta mechanism under that name |
| Budget no longer concentrates on one winner | Widely reported by practitioners; not something ACA measures here |

⛔ **Repeat these as her reading, never as documented platform behaviour.** The practical advice —
build genuinely different ads for genuinely different people — stands regardless of whether the
mechanism is described exactly right, and that is the honest way to carry it.

⚠️ **The course names 2026 explicitly**: *"the brands that win in 2026 and beyond."* It is a dated
claim about a specific algorithm regime and should be checked before a plan is built on it.

## ⚠️ Where this course meets the others

**⛔ Its definition of a concept is not Course 401's.**

| | Components |
|---|---|
| **This course** | **Three**: the offer (what) · the angle (why) · the audience (who) |
| **Course 401**, Creative Strategy Map | **Four**: persona · motivator · benefit · offer |

**Both are Mirella's.** They overlap — persona/audience and offer are common — but 401 splits
*motivator* from *benefit* where this course collapses both into *angle*. ⛔ **Neither course
mentions the other**, and a team using both will produce concept records with different fields.
⭐ **401's is the more useful of the two for briefing**, because separating the emotional driver from
the tangible payoff is exactly what a creative team needs told apart.

**⚠️ Its persona method overlaps Course 302's.** That course's copy-mining expedition and Creative
Translation column produce the same kind of artifact from the same kind of sources.
`aca-build-audience-personas` runs it and emits personas; this course supplies the *micro* framing,
the emotion-clustering step and the burner-account technique, which 302 does not have.

**⚠️ Andromeda also appears in Course 201**, Ashley Vinson's Meta course, framed there as *why
meaningful creative variety matters more than volume*. ⭐ **The two are consistent** — variety over
raw volume is exactly this course's distinction between diversity and 100 variations — but 201
treats it as one update among many and this course builds an operating system on it.

## What is quoted and what is organised

**Everything substantive is Mirella's, quoted directly** — the algorithm account, all three pillars,
the micro-persona example, the source list, the emotion mapping, the burner-account technique and the
four trend lenses.

**This skill's organisation, not ACA's:** the three-file split, the routing table, rendering the
pillars and lenses as tables, and separating the algorithm account from the research method.

**This skill's additions, drawn from the material rather than stated by it:**

- ⛔ **Marking the algorithm claims as a practitioner's reading**, and separating the advice from the
  mechanism so the advice survives if the mechanism is wrong.
- ⭐ **The observation that budget no longer concentrating is the mechanical reason** the course is
  about production systems rather than better single ads.
- ⭐ **The reading that every micro persona is defined by a situation and a posture, never a
  demographic**, which is what makes the footwear example work and the "Sarah, 35" example fail.
- ⭐ **The note that the competitor gap column is the point**, since cataloguing what competitors run
  produces a swipe file and cataloguing what they avoid produces a position.
- ⭐ **The point that ad longevity as a winner signal needs no tooling** — an ad still running after
  months is one somebody keeps paying for.
- ⭐ **Why three- and four-star reviews are the honest middle**, between five-star enthusiasm and
  one-star delivery complaints.
- ⭐ **The observation that every trend lens ends with "record the why"**, and that a reference you
  cannot explain is a screenshot rather than a technique.
- ⭐ **The point that the burner account is the cheapest research method in the programme**, and the
  only one that shows what the persona's feed actually looks like.
- ⛔ **The rule against writing a quote you did not find**, since this method's entire authority comes
  from the quotes being real.
- ⛔ **The comparison of the two concept definitions**, and the argument for 401's.
