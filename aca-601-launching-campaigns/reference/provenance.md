# Provenance

## Source

**Ad Creative Academy** — Course 601: Launching Ad Campaigns (600-Level, Creative Testing). 9m 57s,
1 module. **Mirella Crespi**, Creative Milkshake, ACA's founder.

**Two recordings back it:** a production script and the studio delivery. ⚠️ **They share only about
an eighth of their wording**, so both were read in full and both are carried where they differ.

⭐ **The course code was confirmed in 2026-W33.** Our transcript filenames carry `c600`; ACA re-sent
this course's material under their own filename, *601 - Mirella Crespi - Launching Ad Campaigns*,
matching the held studio recording at **98.8%**. The curriculum already placed it at 601 and ACA has
now confirmed it.

⚠️ **The script's own section header calls this "CREATIVE TESTING & PRODUCTION MANAGEMENT"**, and the
course listing calls it *Launching Ad Campaigns* with a single module titled *Ad Launch Best
Practices*. ⛔ **Neither recording covers ad launch mechanics at all** — no objectives, no budgets, no
ad set structure. The title and the content do not match, and the content is the handover, the
documentation and the volume question.

⚠️ **The script also lists four modules** — Ad Launch Best Practices · Writing Ad Copy for Launch ·
Testing Strategies · Documentation & Naming Conventions — where the shipped course has one. **Nothing
in either recording covers "Writing Ad Copy for Launch."** Treat that outline as a plan that was cut
down, not as a description of what exists.

## ⛔ What ACA ships for this course that is not here

**The *Assess Your Current Creative Needs* framework.** Named in both recordings, on ACA's
tracked-download list, and **not held**. It is the only outstanding gap for this course.

⚠️ **It does something the ladder cannot.** The ladder runs *spend → volume*; that framework runs
*revenue goal → volume* using the account's own historical success rate. The missing input is the hit
rate, and neither recording supplies one.

⭐ **The naming generator gap closed in 2026-W33.** ACA's *Ad Naming Convention Sheet* was listed as
not held; the workbook arrived and is now the source for `aca-name-creative-assets`.

## ⛔ ACA's generator does not implement ACA's convention

**Measured against the workbook.** The course teaches **eleven** naming elements. The sheet has
**eight** dimensions, of which only **five** correspond:

| Taught | In the sheet? |
|---|---|
| Producer · Ad Type · Concept ID · Hook Identifier · Talent Name | ✅ |
| ⛔ **Date · File Name · Concept Stage · Format · Hook Category · VO Type** | **No column** |
| — | Present but never taught: **Product · Asset Length · Landing Page** |

⛔ **Four of the six missing fields are exactly what the course's own four comparative questions
need.** *Are image ads working better than video ads* needs **Format**. *Is UGC working better than
studio production* needs **Producer**, which is present. *Are ads with VO better than ads with just
music* needs **VO Type**. *Are funny hooks working better than problem-first hooks* needs **Hook
Category**. **Three of the four questions cannot be answered by the sheet ACA ships to answer them.**

## Scope

The creative-to-media handover, naming, documentation and creative volume. Not campaign setup,
objectives, budgets or ad set structure — the course says outright that account structure is not the
strategist's role.
