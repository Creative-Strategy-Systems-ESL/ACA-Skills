# Provenance

## Source

**Ad Creative Academy** — Course 103: Creative Fundamentals (100-Level), taught by **Mirella
Crespi**, founder of Ad Creative Academy and of Creative Milkshake. 19 minutes 46 seconds, 1
module: The Performance Framework.

## The materials this skill carries

- **The 10 Types of Angles Worksheet** — all ten angles with core message, description, when to
  use, psychological triggers and example applications. Reproduced in `angle-types.md`.
- **The Ad Building Blocks Analysis Worksheet** — the five-section teardown. Reproduced in
  `worksheet.md` as a runnable structure.
- **AI Prompts for Creative Analysis & Development** — 6 prompts plus 3 strategic workflows.
- **AI Prompt for Ad Building Blocks Analysis** — the standalone mega-prompt that teaches the
  framework to an AI before requesting the analysis. Reproduced whole in `ai-prompts.md`.

The course also ships the ACA 2025 Creative Strategy Presentation Template and a Building Blocks
Analysis assignment, which are not carried here.

## ⚠️ Three inconsistencies inside ACA's own material

Recorded rather than silently corrected, so anyone comparing this skill against the source can see
why they differ.

**1. A mis-titled prompt.** In the Creative Analysis & Development pack, the second prompt is
headed "Audience Pain Point Mining", but its body is a building-block deconstruction and has
nothing to do with pain point mining. ACA's own workflows refer to it as the "Building Block
Identification" prompt. Named for what it does.

**2. Two workflows share a title.** Workflows 1 and 2 are both headed "Competitor Analysis Cycle".
Workflow 2's content is performance diagnosis, not competitor analysis. Retitled here as
"Performance Diagnosis Cycle".

**3. A section stamped with a different course.** The Strategic Workflows pages inside the Course
103 prompt pack carry the header **"Course 704: AI Powered Iterations & Creative Improvement"**.
They ship with 103 and are included here for that reason, with the stamp noted.

Separately, the 10 Types of Angles Worksheet exists in two exports, one stamped Course 103 and one
stamped Course 401. Course 103's transcript names it as a 103 resource, and ACA's shipping
curriculum lists it under both, so it genuinely belongs to both.

## What is quoted and what is organised

Framework contents are ACA's, reproduced: the three-part structure and its hook techniques, all 14
building blocks across three categories, the three proven block combinations, the four-step block
library process, the five real-world success patterns, the four-step reverse engineering process,
the 15 vocabulary terms, all 10 angle types, and the worksheet's five sections.

The symptom-to-block diagnostic table, the notes on which angles are commonly confused, and the
guidance on when a framework fails in practice are this skill's organisation, not the course's
structure.
