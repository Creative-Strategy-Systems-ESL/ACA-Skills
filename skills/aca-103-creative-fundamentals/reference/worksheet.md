# The Ad Building Blocks Analysis Worksheet

The artifact this course produces: a structured teardown of one ad. Five sections.

> "By breaking down ads into their core components, you'll develop the ability to identify what
> makes great creative work and how to replicate success."

**Analyse one ad at a time.** The worksheet asks for timestamps and per-block effectiveness
ratings, which is only possible against a specific piece of creative. If someone wants a whole
competitor library assessed, run the sheet per ad and compare the formulas at the end.

⚠️ **Rate what is there, not what you would have done.** The effectiveness scores are about whether
the block works in this ad, for this audience. A slick demo that interrupts the emotional arc can
score low even though it is well made.

## Section 4 — Overall Analysis & Insights

- **Block combination assessment:** which blocks work particularly well together in this ad?
- **Missing blocks:** which blocks are notably absent that might have strengthened it?
- **Modular replication potential:** which specific blocks or combinations could be reused
  elsewhere?
- **Structure effectiveness:** how well does the Hook-Body-CTA structure work here? What could be
  improved?
- **Key performance drivers:** which 2-3 blocks contribute most to this ad's effectiveness?
- **Modular improvement suggestions:** how could this ad be improved by modifying, adding or
  rearranging specific blocks?

The **missing blocks** question is the one that most often produces the useful finding. It is
easier to see what an ad does than what it declines to do, and the absent block is frequently the
opportunity.

## What to do with a completed sheet

The formula in Section 5 is the transferable output. The ad itself is not reusable, and copying it
produces a worse version of someone else's creative. The block sequence is reusable, and running it
against a different offer, angle and audience is the actual technique.

Analysing three or four ads from the same competitor and comparing their formulas is more
informative than analysing one in depth: the repeated blocks reveal what they have found works,
and the blocks none of them use reveal what nobody in the category is trying.
