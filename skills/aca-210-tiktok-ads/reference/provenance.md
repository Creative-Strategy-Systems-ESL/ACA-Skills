# Provenance

## Source

**Ad Creative Academy** — Course 210: TikTok Ads Essentials (200-Level, Platforms & Channels).
13m 44s, 1 module: *Campaign Objectives & Creative Analysis*.

**Mirella Crespi**, Creative Milkshake, ACA's founder.

⭐ **The shortest course in the programme**, and the only platform course outside Meta. ~1,850 words
of source, but unusually dense — thirteen numbered sections, most of them a framework.

**No worksheet ships with it.** ACA's tracked-download list runs to 46 rows covering 45 distinct assets, and the entire
200-Level has exactly one, which belongs to Course 230.

## ⚠️ The source is a production script, not a delivered recording

The filed artifact is the script the module was made from. Two things follow:

1. **It ends with an unfilled placeholder** — *"I'm [NAME]. Go create something impossible to swipe
   away."* — which is the clearest possible sign of what this document is.
2. **The delivered recording may differ.** Where a script and a delivery disagree, the delivery is
   what students actually heard. This skill carries the script because it is what exists.

## ⚠️ How old this material is

Better anchored than the other 200-Level courses, because the course dates itself:

- **Section 5 is titled "How the 2025 Algorithm Decides Your Fate."** Algorithm behaviour and
  ranking pillars are as of that point.
- It cites **Kantar's 2021** cross-platform sound study.
- It was received into this library in **2026-W33** — read as a "no later than", not a recording
  date. ACA stamps no recording date on the course.

⛔ **Do not present the tooling as current.** Symphony, TikTok One, Generate & Remix, Avatar Videos,
Translate & Dub, Daily Video Generations, Creative Center's Top Ads Spotlight and CapCut templates
are named as they were. Product names and capabilities move faster here than anywhere else in the
programme.

## ⚠️ Sections 8 and 9 do not exist

The script's numbering runs **1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, then 10, 11, 12, 13.** Sections 8 and 9 are
absent.

**Verified in both filed copies of this module**, so it is not a filing artefact — it is how ACA's
script is numbered. Whether two sections were cut or the numbering simply jumped is not knowable
from the document.

**Consequence for anyone using this:** there may be material the course intended to carry that is
not here. Nothing in sections 1 to 7 or 10 to 13 refers forward or back to a missing 8 or 9, so no
framework is visibly broken — but do not assume this is the complete module.

⚠️ **Related, and a trap when reading the script:** the heading **"10 Production Best Practices"** is
**section 10 containing five practices**, not a list of ten. Same for **"12 Six Day Action Plan"**,
which is section 12 and does contain six days.

## ⭐ Where this course was found, which is why it exists at all

Course 210 was recorded as missing and awaiting upload. It was not missing.

A file named for **Courses 702 and 703** turned out to be **98.5% this TikTok module** — 1,800 of
its 1,827 body words. The 702 and 703 content it was named for amounted to **27 words**: two bare
headings and one sentence. An earlier pass had cut Mirella's omnibus script at her own "702:"
heading and run to the end of the document, so the TikTok module that followed came along with it
and was filed under someone else's course code.

⚠️ **This is one of two modules found bundled into the wrong course's file** in this programme; the
other is Course 201's Andromeda module, which sits at the back of the Course 202 export. Worth
knowing if you are matching this material against ACA's own files.

## What is quoted and what is organised

**Everything substantive is Mirella's, quoted directly.** The script is written prose rather than
speech, so quotes need almost no tidying.

**This skill's organisation:** the four-file split, the routing table, and the grouping of sections
4, 5, 6, 11 and 12 into one operational file. The course's own section order is preserved within
each file.

**This skill's additions, drawn from the material rather than stated by it:**

- The warning that the **sound-on rule here conflicts with the never-assume-audio rule** in ACA's
  Meta course. Both are correct for their platform; carrying one across is a real error and neither
  course flags it.
- The note that **the 20-35 second sweet spot conflicts with Meta's under-six-seconds guidance**,
  for the same reason.
- The note that **Moments have the shortest shelf life** of the Force/Moment/Signal triad, and that
  the example Moment given will be long dead.
- The warning that **sub-culture language cannot be invented** — the drill requires actually
  watching the community's content, and inventing its inside jokes is the failure the technique
  exists to avoid.
