# Provenance

## Source

**Ad Creative Academy** — Course 403: Storyboarding (400-Level, Creative Strategy & Ideation).
13m 26s, 1 module. **Mirella Crespi**, Creative Milkshake, ACA's founder.

Two assets underpin this skill:

1. **The Storyboard Template**, as an 8-page PDF
2. ⭐ **ACA Storyboard Template 2025**, the working `.xlsx` — 8 sheets, and the source of record for
   every column name in `reference/the-storyboard.md`

## ⛔ What ACA ships that this skill does not have

**AI Storyboard Prompts.** ACA's tracked-download list names it under this course and it is not in
this library. ACA writes it *"Al Storyboard Prompts"* on their own sheet, with a lowercase L.

**So this skill is not the complete Course 403 toolkit**, and should not be presented as one. What
is missing is a prompt pack for generating storyboards with AI; what is here is the template itself
and the method. If someone needs the prompts, say they exist and are not in this folder.

⚠️ **The teaching does not depend on them.** The transcript names AI writing assistants generically
(Copy.ai, Claude, ChatGPT) rather than routing to that pack, so nothing in the method has a hole in
it.

## ⭐ The template in this skill is not the one this library held until 2026-W33

This matters because it changes what the skill is built on.

An earlier filed copy of "Storyboard Template" turned out to be a **fragment**: 42 distinct words,
one grid, against the real asset's 8 pages and 162 distinct words. **Only 22% of the real template
appeared in it.** A skill built on the fragment would have had the Ad Breakdown grid and none of the
scene table, the Graphic Ads sheet, the Hook Factory, the building blocks or the copy formulas.

⚠️ **The fragment was also filed under the wrong course**, as a 500-Level asset, because the
transcript that names storyboards most often is the 500 section. ACA's own list puts the Storyboard
Template under 403.

**Everything in `reference/the-storyboard.md` comes from the full `.xlsx`**, read sheet by sheet.

## ⚠️ Two defects in the shipped workbook

1. **The Overview grid's rows C2 to C8 contain `#REF!` errors.** Only C1 is a worked example. This
   is in the file ACA distributes, not an extraction artifact.
2. **The PDF version loses its column names to multi-column text extraction**, producing interleaved
   character runs. The `.xlsx` is the reliable source and is what this skill used.

## ⛔ Fifteen building blocks here, fourteen in Course 103

The workbook's Building Blocks sheet lists **15** blocks: Attention · Interest / Intro Product ·
Desire · Proof · Action · Problem · Agitate · Discredit · Solution · Benefits · Barriers · Scarcity ·
Offer · Credibility · Before.

**Course 103 teaches 14**, grouped Structure / Product / Person, under different names.

**These are two taxonomies from the same programme and this skill does not merge them.** The
template's dropdown is built from the 15, so filling the template uses the 15. Where
`aca-103-creative-fundamentals` is installed alongside this one, say the names differ and map by
meaning rather than assuming a shared vocabulary.

## What is quoted and what is organised

**Everything substantive is Mirella's, quoted directly.** The transcript is written prose from a
studio script, so quotes need almost no tidying.

**Column names, sheet names and the Building Blocks descriptions are reproduced verbatim from the
workbook**, emoji included, because they are the join between this skill's output and ACA's file.

**This skill's organisation, not ACA's:** the four-file split, the routing table, presenting the
storyboard-versus-brief choice as the first decision, and rendering each sheet as a fillable table
rather than describing it.

**This skill's additions, drawn from the material rather than stated by it:**

- ⛔ **The refusal to invent talent, props, outfit or location**, on the grounds that those are
  production commitments with money attached and get bought from this document. ACA does not warn
  about it.
- ⭐ **Reading the Overview's consolidated props and styling as the shoot-day shopping list**, which
  is what makes filling every concept row worth the effort.
- ⭐ **Naming the Hook Factory as the cheapest production in the programme**, and the routing rule:
  when someone has a proven ad and is about to brief a new concept, send them here first.
- **The point that five hook rows against one body is what makes one shoot yield five testable
  ads.**
- **The observation that Mirella's failed-script remedy sends you back to the concept, not to the
  wording** — a script failing the benefit question does not have a copy problem.
- **The instruction to run question 3 cold**, as a viewer who has not read the brief.
- **The note that reading time, not just grade level, is what the Hemingway step is for**, and the
  three things that usually push a script above Grade 5.
- **The warning that supers are not the script copied across**, and why they are a separate column.
- ⛔ **The fixed-versus-flexible table** in `briefing-a-creator.md`, and the point that an unstated
  boundary is discovered by being crossed. ACA names the item; the contents are this skill's.
- **The two failure modes of a brief**, too tight and too loose, and what each costs.
- **The framing that a brief is a different instrument rather than a storyboard with fields
  missing.**
- **The note that AUDIO LINK and VIDEO LINK are post-shoot tracking** and should be left empty in a
  delivered storyboard.
- **The reading of AIDPA versus PADSA as a choice about where the ad starts** — the product's appeal
  or the viewer's pain — and that the choice belongs in the STRATEGY column.

## ⚠️ How old this material is

ACA stamps no recording date. Received into this library in **2026-W31** for the transcript and
**2026-W33** for the full template, both a "no later than".

⭐ **The template dates slowly.** Columns, sheets, building blocks and copy formulas do not depend on
any platform behaviour, and the workbook names itself "2025".

⛔ **The named tools date fastest**: Copy.ai, Claude, ChatGPT, **TikTok Symphony's script generator**
and the **Hemingway App**. Symphony in particular is a platform feature rather than a product.
**The Grade 5 test survives any of them disappearing.**

⚠️ **The course names no benchmark numbers**, which is consistent with the rest of the 400-Level.
Any threshold attributed to it would be invented. The two real numbers in it are *Grade 5 reading
level* and *10+ hooks per winning concept*, and both are ACA's own instructions rather than measured
results.

## Scope

The **document**, not the craft or the shoot. It does not cover developing the concept (Course 401),
casting or directing talent (Course 502), production logistics, editing, or media buying.

Mirella closes by pointing forward: *"In our next module, we'll explore how to optimize your
production process to create more assets with less time and budget."*
