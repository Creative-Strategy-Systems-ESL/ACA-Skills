# Provenance

## Source

**Ad Creative Academy** — Course 101: Tribe Building on Social Media (100-Level), taught by **Seth
Godin (Akimbo)**. 48 minutes 24 seconds, 6 modules.

ACA's curriculum lists **10 case studies** for this course. `worked-examples.md` carries **9**:
the cases Godin develops far enough in the recording to reason from. Some brands he names in
passing (Patagonia, the bakery people queue for, American Express) are mentioned as evidence rather
than worked through, so they appear inside the argument instead of as standalone cases. The
difference between 10 and 9 is a counting convention, not a missing case.

Delivered as a masterclass. Godin introduces himself as the author of 22 consecutive bestsellers,
translated into almost 40 languages, the inventor of email marketing, and the volunteer organiser
of the Carbon Almanac.

## The six modules, as ACA ships them

1. Earning Attention & Trust (9m 14s)
2. Brand Advertising vs Direct Response Advertising (7m 00s)
3. Creating Culture and Stories Worth Sharing (10m 21s)
4. The Smallest Viable Audience (5m 24s)
5. Building an Authentic Voice (4m 55s)
6. Ad Positioning (11m 30s)

⚠️ **The recording is numbered differently from the module list.** On tape Godin marks five
sections, and they do not line up with the six shipped modules:

| Shipped module | Where it sits on the recording |
|---|---|
| 1. Earning Attention & Trust | Section 2, "Interruption vs permission marketing" |
| 2. Brand Advertising vs Direct Response | Section 1, same name |
| 3. Creating Culture and Stories Worth Sharing | Section 4, same name |
| 4. The Smallest Viable Audience | Inside section 3 |
| 5. Building an Authentic Voice | Section 5 |
| 6. Ad Positioning | Inside section 5 |

Recording section 3, **"Why most social media ads are a waste of time"**, has no corresponding
shipped module, and it is the argumentative spine of the course. Anyone looking for module 4 or 6
as standalone segments will not find them under those names.

## What is quoted and what is organised

Quoted passages throughout are Godin's own words from the delivered recording. The organisation
into argument, frameworks, examples and questions is this skill's work, not his structure. Where a
phrasing is doing the work of a whole idea ("a brand is a promise", "authenticity is a crock",
"they were just a bunch", "positioning is a generous act"), it is quoted rather than paraphrased,
because the compression is the point.

## One thing to be careful about

This course argues **against** heavy paid social spending, inside a paid social certification. That
is not an error to be smoothed over, and Godin states it in his opening: *"Turns out most social
media ads are a waste of time. As someone who has spent and lost millions of dollars buying online
ads."* Represent the position as he makes it.

Equally, do not overstate it into "never advertise". He built ad businesses, he credits direct
response with real results, and his actual claim is about the ceiling built into measurable
interruption, not about advertising being worthless.
