# Provenance

## Source

**Ad Creative Academy** — Course 102: State of Paid Social Advertising & Consumer Psychology
(100-Level), taught by **Mirella Crespi**, founder of Ad Creative Academy and of Creative
Milkshake, a performance creative studio. 27 minutes 13 seconds, 2 modules:

1. The New Paid Social Reality (9m 41s)
2. Data-Driven Creative Strategy (17m 32s)

## The materials this skill carries

- **The Creative Strategist's Consumer Psychology Worksheet**, ACA's campaign planning guide for
  this course. Reproduced in `worksheet.md` as a runnable structure: all 8 sections, every field,
  in ACA's order.
- **AI Prompts for Creative Strategists: Consumer Psychology Edition**, 20 prompts. Reproduced
  verbatim in `ai-prompts.md` with placeholders intact.

## Credits the course gives

- **Abraham Maslow** — the hierarchy of needs
- **Robert Cialdini** — the principles of persuasion. The course teaches his seven and adds seven
  more from other psychological research
- **Eugene Schwartz** — the 5 levels of market sophistication, invoked in Prompt 3

The **Life-Force 8** is presented as an established framework of hard-wired human drives; the
course does not attribute it to a named author.

## ⚠️ One discrepancy inside ACA's own material

**The teaching and the worksheet disagree on how many persuasion principles there are.**

The course script teaches **14**: Cialdini's seven (reciprocity, commitment and consistency, social
proof, authority, liking, scarcity, unity), then MAYA, loss aversion, anchoring, pratfall effect,
distinctiveness, self-centeredness, and primacy and recency.

The worksheet's Section 3 selection grid offers **10**, omitting MAYA, distinctiveness,
self-centeredness, and primacy and recency.

This skill carries all 14 and flags which four the worksheet leaves out, so a student working from
the printed sheet can see what is missing rather than assuming the grid is the complete set.
Neither source is wrong; the worksheet is a selection aid, not an index.

## What is quoted and what is organised

Framework contents (the 5 awareness stages and their tactical approaches, the Life-Force 8 and
their explanations, all 14 principles, the 4-exposure sequence, the worksheet's 8 sections and its
10-item checklist) are ACA's, reproduced. The two worked examples are Mirella's.

The grouping into four layers, the guidance on filling order, and the notes on how the frameworks
fail in practice are this skill's organisation, not the course's structure.
