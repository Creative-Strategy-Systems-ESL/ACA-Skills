# Research trends and turn one into an ad

**Ad Creative Academy**, Course 302 (Mirella Crespi), module 4.

## ⛔ Read this before anything else, because it inverts the usual goal

> "the research puzzle that many brands completely miss trends research.The goal here isn't to chase
> every trend **we want to create everygreen ads that can scale.** The goal is to understand the
> underlying patterns of what's working on each platform so your ads feel native and relevant **you
> want to blend in AND stand out.**"

**This is trend research in service of evergreen creative, not trend-jacking.** An answer that helps
someone move faster on a viral sound has inverted the module.

## What this skill needs from you

**Required:**

1. **The trend**, described or linked. 2 to 3 examples of it, because one example is a video and
   three is a pattern
2. **The brand** — what it sells, who buys it, and what it stands for. The relevance test is the
   whole decision and it cannot run without this

**Optional, and each sharpens it:**

3. The platform the ad is destined for
4. The brand's tolerance for lo-fi or unbranded creative, which is often the real constraint
5. How fast production can move, which decides whether a Moment is even reachable
6. Any trend the brand has tried before and how it landed

⚠️ **If no specific trend is supplied**, the job is monitoring rather than adaptation. Use the weekly
system in `reference/the-prompts-and-workflow.md` and say that is what you are giving.

⛔ **If the brand is missing, do not skip to the template.** The relevance test in step 2 is the only
thing standing between a trend adaptation and a brand embarrassing itself, and it is the step people
want to skip.

## The decision, before the craft

**First, place the trend**, because the level sets the budget:

| Level | Life | What it justifies |
|---|---|---|
| **Moments** | 1 to 4 weeks | Act within **48 to 72 hours** or not at all. ⚠️ Mirella advises against investing heavily here for **ads** — *"honestly better for your organic strategy"* |
| **Signals** | 1 to 6 months | Campaign themes running 1 to 3 months |
| **Forces** | 6+ months | Overall creative strategy. Worth **significant production investment** |

⭐ **Most trend mistakes are a level mismatch**, not a taste failure: a production budget spent on a
Moment, or a Force treated as a passing fad. Place it first.

**Then test relevance four ways** — behavioural, cultural, psychological, emotional. A trend that
passes none of them is a costume, not a creative opportunity. `reference/the-worksheet.md`.

**Only then build the template.**

## How to run it

⛔ **The inversion above governs every step.** The goal is deciding whether a trend is worth using, not
finding one to use.

1. **Name the trend and where it was seen.** A trend nobody can point at is a hunch.
2. ⛔ **Place it as a Moment, a Signal or a Force** using `reference/the-worksheet.md`. The three have
   different shelf lives and different correct responses, and skipping this is how a Moment gets treated as a
   Force.
3. ⛔ **Run the relevance test before the craft**, per the decision section below. ⭐ **A defensible "do not
   use this" is a successful outcome of this skill**, not a failure to produce.
4. **Only if it passes, work the prompts** in `reference/the-prompts-and-workflow.md`.
5. **Emit the worksheet** from `reference/the-worksheet.md`, filled, including the verdict and its reasoning.
6. **Rebuild it as a reusable template**, not a one-off ad, which is what makes the research worth the time.
7. **Check the verdict has a reason attached.** "It is trending" is the input, never the conclusion.

## Answering well

**Structure transfers, content does not.** *"You're looking for patterns you can adapt, not specific
content to copy. The goal is to understand **why** something is resonating."*

⭐ **Audio is a first-class component here and almost nowhere else.** ACA's trend template names
**Sound** alongside Hook, Deliver and Guide. From the delivered session: *"Oftentimes if we just
change a voiceover from a human voiceover to that TikTok robot voice, sometimes it'll make a
difference in how the creative performs."* Do not leave the audio row blank.

**Check the shelf life before recommending anything.** A trend is dated the moment it is written
down. Any specific trend named in this skill's material is an example of the method, not a
recommendation — and by the time anyone reads it, most will be dead.

**Native means made for, not resized for.** The three principles Mirella gives for Reels and TikTok:
make it **entertaining**, make it **digestible**, make it **relatable**.

⚠️ **ACA's script and delivery give different triads here.** The written recap says *"Educational,
Entertaining, Aspirational"* and never defines them. The delivered session defines entertaining,
digestible, relatable. **Use the delivered set** — it is what students heard and the only one the
course explains. See `reference/provenance.md`.
