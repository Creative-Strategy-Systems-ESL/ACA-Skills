# The prompts and the weekly workflow

ACA ships **"AI Prompts & Workflows For Trends Research"**: five prompts in two groups, plus a
monitoring system.

## Trend Identification prompts

| # | Prompt | What it is for |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Platform-Specific Trend Analysis** | Reading what is currently moving on a given platform |
| **2** | **Platform-Specific Trend Analysis** (second variant) | ⚠️ ACA gives two prompts under the same heading. They are different prompts; the shared title is theirs |
| **3** | **Trend Lifecycle Assessment** | ⭐ Placing a trend as a Moment, Signal or Force |

⭐ **Prompt 3 is the one that decides the budget**, and therefore the most consequential of the five.
It is the automated version of step 0.

## ⛔ The reliability problem, which ACA does not raise

**These prompts ask a model about what is trending right now.** That is the single least reliable
thing a model can be asked, for two reasons that compound:

1. **Trends are the fastest-moving information there is.** A Moment lives 1 to 4 weeks. Any model
   answering from training data is describing a dead trend with total confidence.
2. **Trend names are highly generatable.** A plausible-sounding format name, sound and structure can
   be produced for any category on demand, and it will read exactly like a real one.

⛔ **So treat every prompt output as a hypothesis to verify on the platform, never as a finding.**

| Prompt | Reliability | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **1 and 2**, identifying current trends | ⚠️ **Lowest.** ⛔ Verify every named trend on the platform before acting | Claims about the world right now |
| **3**, lifecycle assessment | ⚠️ Medium. Sound if you supply real observation dates; guesswork if not | Reasoning over evidence you provide |
| **4**, structure breakdown | ✅ **Highest.** You paste the examples and it analyses them | Works on material you supply |
| **5**, brand adaptation | ✅ Good, and inherits whatever came in from 4 | Generation, not claims about the world |

⭐ **The pattern is the same one that runs through this whole course: prompts that work on material
you supply are reliable, and prompts that make claims about the world are not.** Prompts 4 and 5 are
the useful half.

**How to check a named trend, in about a minute:** search the format or sound on the platform, look at
post dates on the top results, and check whether anything is from the last two weeks. If the recent
results are thin, the trend is over or was never real.

## The weekly trend monitoring system

ACA's three-step system, and the part worth keeping even if the prompts are not used.

### 1. Set up dedicated research accounts

Separate accounts, per platform, used only for research.

⭐ **This is the highest-value item in the entire module and it costs nothing.** Your personal feed has
been trained on you: it shows a marketer marketer-content, which is the least representative sample
of the customer available. A clean account trained by deliberate engagement on the target audience's
content is a genuine research instrument.

⚠️ **It only works if you feed it deliberately.** An account you scroll idly becomes a second personal
feed within a fortnight.

### 2. Use AI for initial scanning

Fast, wide, unreliable. Read the reliability table above before trusting anything it names.

### 3. Weekly analysis with AI

Structured review of what was captured — where prompt 4 does the real work, on examples you saved
yourself.

**Paired with the module's own action steps:**

> "Schedule three 30-minute 'strategic scrolling' sessions this week. Use our simplified trends
> worksheet to analyze what you find. Identify one trend that could be relevant to your brand. Draft
> a quick trend template mapping your brand story to the trend structure."

⭐ **90 minutes a week, and one trend at the end of it.** That ratio is the honest version of trend
research, and it is worth quoting to anyone expecting a pipeline of them.

---

## Running the workflow well

**Save the example, not the description.** Trend content disappears, and a note saying "that transition
one with the beat drop" is unusable in a fortnight.

**Log the date you first saw it.** Prompt 3 cannot place a trend on the Moment/Signal/Force scale
without knowing how long it has been running, and that is the input people fail to supply.

**Most weeks produce nothing.** The system's value is that it is running when something does appear,
not that it yields a trend every Friday. A week with no adaptable trend is a normal week, not a
failed one.
