# Provenance

## Source

**Ad Creative Academy** — two assets shipped with **Course 302: Research for Creative Strategy**,
module 4, taught by **Mirella Crespi** of Creative Milkshake, ACA's founder:

1. **Trends Research Worksheet**, which she calls the *"Simplified Trends Research Worksheet"*
2. **AI Prompts & Workflows For Trends Research** — 5 prompts and a weekly monitoring system

The module also points at its own presentation slides (*"How To Use Trends"* and *"Trend Template"*),
which are referenced in the recording and are not in this library. **The frameworks on them are
described in the narration**, which is where this skill's versions come from, so nothing is missing
in substance. But the visual originals are not held.

## ⛔ ACA's script and delivery give different platform principles

The written script's on-screen recap says:

> "Deploy the 3 pillars — **Educational, Entertaining, Aspirational**"

**Those three are never defined anywhere in the module.** The delivered studio session instead gives:

> "remember these three key principles. One, **make it entertaining.** Use music, visual,
> storytelling to provoke an emotional response. Make it **digestible** and easy to understand.
> Reels and TikTok are fast. You have to keep it short and to the point. Use emojis and text to help
> convey your message. And last, make it **relatable.** Use real people and content creators to
> create content that is authentic in the low-fi tone."

**Only *entertaining* appears in both.** This skill uses the delivered set: it is what students heard
and the only one the course explains. Flagged rather than silently reconciled.

## ⚠️ The Moments / Signals / Forces framework is not ACA's

Mirella credits it to **TikTok** explicitly: *"a framework developed by TikTok."* Carry that
attribution.

⚠️ **The same framework is taught in ACA's own TikTok course** (Course 210). The two agree. Where that
skill is installed alongside this one, either can carry it.

## ⚠️ The PDFs stamp a different course number than the curriculum does

Both assets footer themselves *"Course 301: Research for Creative Strategy"*. ACA's shipping
curriculum numbers this course **302**; 301 is Zach Murray's Reverse Engineering Winning Ads. ACA
renumbered for launch and the worksheets were stamped before that. This skill uses 302.

## What is reproduced and what is this skill's

**Reproduced as written:** the three trend levels with their durations and investment guidance, the
three-step framework with all four identification methods and all four relevance tests, the five key
elements, the Hook/Deliver/Guide/Sound template, the simplified four-step loop, all seven organic
formats with ACA's reason for each, the case study, the five prompts by name, and the three-step
weekly system.

**This skill's, drawn from the material rather than stated by it:**

- ⛔ **The reliability ranking of the five prompts**, and the rule that prompts making claims about
  what is trending now are the least trustworthy thing in the pack. ACA presents all five as
  equivalent and does not warn about confabulated trend names anywhere.
- **The one-minute platform check** for verifying a named trend before acting on it.
- ⭐ **The observation that the pattern holds across the whole course**: prompts that work on material
  you supply are reliable, and prompts that make claims about the world are not.
- ⭐ **The reading of the trend levels as a budgeting tool**, and that **most trend failures are a
  level mismatch** rather than a taste failure.
- ⭐ **Naming the dedicated research account as the highest-value item in the module**, with the reason
  ACA does not give: a personal feed has been trained on you and shows a marketer marketer-content,
  which is the least representative sample of the customer available. And the caveat that an account
  scrolled idly becomes a second personal feed within a fortnight.
- **The point that 2 to 3 examples is the minimum for a pattern**, since one example is a video.
- **The reading of the case study as "invert the emotional payload, keep the structure"**, which is
  the transferable move rather than the 42%.
- **The observation that the seven organic formats double as a coverage checklist**, and that unlike
  live trends none of them will be dead next month.
- ⚠️ **The note that passing one relevance test weakly is not passing**, and that a "no" at step 2 is
  a legitimate and valuable deliverable.
- **The caution that volume of posts is not evidence of performance**, which is the same limit that
  applies to competitor ads.
- **The instruction to save the example rather than a description of it**, and to log the date first
  seen, since prompt 3 cannot place a trend without it.
- **The point that most weeks produce nothing**, and that this is the system working rather than
  failing.

## ⚠️ The claims are ACA's

| Claim | As given |
|---|---|
| Beauty brand, trend flip | **42% higher CTR** than their previous creative |
| Voiceover swap | Changing a human voiceover to the TikTok robot voice *"sometimes we'll make a difference"* |
| Platform mechanics | Reels and TikTok algorithms are *"all about discovery"* |

⚠️ **The 42% is unsourced, concerns one unnamed client, and is a CTR result** — the metric furthest
from revenue. The same course warns elsewhere against judging creative on engagement rather than
business results, so hold both.

## ⛔ Everything named in this module dates faster than anything else in the programme

**This is the module with the shortest shelf life in ACA's entire curriculum**, by its own logic: a
Moment lives 1 to 4 weeks.

- **The named trends** — *"My wife has two husbands"*, *"what I ordered vs. what I got"*, *"we listen
  and we don't judge"* — are **certainly dead**. They are examples of the method, not
  recommendations, and repeating them as current would be a visible error.
- **The seven organic formats** are the durable half. They completed the journey to Force and are now
  permanent ad formats rather than trends.
- **The tools** — TikTok and Meta Creative Center — are named as free and current. Access moves.
- **The framework itself** is the most durable thing here and does not depend on any specific trend.

ACA stamps no date on the assets. They were received into this library in **2026-W31**, which is a
"no later than" rather than a publication date.

## Deliberate overlap with two other skills

Each must run with nothing else installed.

- **`aca-302-research-for-creative-strategy`** teaches all four research passes and explains why
  trends sit last, after the account, the audience and the competitors.
- **This skill** runs pass 4 on a specific trend for a specific brand.
- **ACA's Course 210 (TikTok Ads Essentials)** carries the same Moments/Signals/Forces framework
  alongside platform mechanics: objectives, formats, and the metric thresholds this course
  deliberately does not give. **Route platform mechanics there**, and keep this one for the question
  of whether a trend is worth building an ad around.
