# Course 230: Cross Channel Strategies

**Carlotta Costanzo**, GAIN. 33m 08s, 3 modules, 9 case studies.

The course about **orchestration** — how platforms and funnel stages fit together, rather than how
any one of them works.

> "Your customers aren't sticking to just one channel. They're scrolling through social media,
> searching on Google, networking on LinkedIn, and listening to music on YouTube. That's why
> multi-channel strategies are not optional — they're essential."

## What this skill needs from you

**Required: none.** Ask a cross-channel question and get an answer.

**To run the AI workflow and produce adapted messaging, required:**

1. **The brand**, and what makes it different from the obvious alternatives
2. **The audience**, and what you already know about them

**Optional, and it sharpens everything:**

3. Which channels are running now, and how budget currently splits
4. Whether this is a peak trading moment or steady state
5. The core message, if one already exists

## The three principles the course ends on

> **1. Be where your customers are.** They move between platforms seamlessly; effective campaigns
> meet them on their terms.
>
> **2. Align creative with context.** Tailor format and messaging to both the journey stage **and**
> the platform's strengths.
>
> **3. Stay consistent, but evolve your story.** Anchor to one core message; change how you tell it
> as people move from awareness to consideration to conversion.

⭐ **Principle 3 is the one that does the work**, and the one people get wrong in both directions —
either running identical creative everywhere, or running four unrelated campaigns that share a logo.

## How to run it

**Channel choice is downstream of funnel stage**, and taking it in the other order is the mistake the course
is written against.

1. **Make the multi-channel case** from `reference/the-case-and-the-balance.md`, including push against pull
   and its seasonal rhythm. Skipping this makes the rest sound like more work for its own sake.
2. **Give the four platform personalities** in `reference/platform-personalities.md` — what each is actually
   good at, rather than what each claims.
3. **Map funnel stage to channel** with `reference/funnel-mapping.md`, and attach the metric that belongs to
   each stage. ⛔ The metrics are the part people drop, and dropping them is how a mid-funnel channel gets
   judged on last-click.
4. **Adapt one core message across channels and stages**, using the worked examples in the same file rather
   than inventing new ones.
5. **Run the four-step AI workflow** in `reference/ai-workflow.md` if they want it operationalised.
6. ⭐ **For the Cross-Vertical Inspiration Finder, route to `aca-find-cross-vertical-inspiration`** — it has
   its own skill, noted below. ⚠️ Confirm it is installed before naming it.
7. **Check they can say which channel does which job** on their own account. A channel list with no jobs
   attached means step 3 did not land.

## Answering well

**Push and pull are not either/or.** The course's most useful correction: Booking.com is the pull
example and Dr. Squatch the push example, and *both brands do both.* The question is the balance and
what it should be right now.

⭐ **The seasonal rhythm is the actionable part.** Ramp push before and during a peak to build
demand, then let pull capture the high-intent audience that creates — *"often at higher efficiency
and lower cost."* Someone planning Black Friday should hear this.

**Do not map one platform to one funnel stage.** The course is explicit that while platforms have
natural strengths, *"the most effective cross-channel strategies use all relevant platforms
throughout the journey"*, with content and objectives tailored per stage.

**Metrics change by stage, and judging a stage by the wrong metric is the standard failure.**
Awareness work measured on ROAS will always look like it failed.

different job.

## ⚠️ Scope

This course is about **orchestration across channels**. It is not a how-to for any single platform —
for Meta or TikTok specifically, ACA has dedicated courses, and if `aca-201-meta-ads` or
`aca-210-tiktok-ads` are installed alongside this skill, route platform mechanics to them.

It gives no budget-split percentages, no channel benchmarks, and no attribution methodology beyond
naming multi-channel attribution as *"the beauty and the complexity"*.
