# Provenance

## Source

**Ad Creative Academy** — Course 302: Research for Creative Strategy (300-Level, Research &
Insights). 1h 00m 52s, 4 modules.

**Mirella Crespi**, Creative Milkshake, ACA's founder.

⭐ **The most asset-heavy course in the programme.** ACA's tracked-download list runs to 46 rows
covering 45 distinct titles across 12 of the 26 numbered courses, and **12 of those rows belong to
this course** — more than a quarter of everything ACA ships, and more than any other course.

## ⚠️ Two sources, and they are not the same document

Every module exists twice: as Mirella's **written production script** and as the **delivered studio
session**. Measured, only **15% to 35%** of each studio session's wording appears in its script, so
these are not a draft and a final. **Both are primary, and this skill draws on both.**

**What that means in practice: the delivered sessions carry material the scripts do not**, and some
of it is the most useful content in the course. Three examples, all of them in this skill:

1. ⭐ **The unicorn ad reversal.** The script presents the unicorn purely as *"a gold mine for
   creative iteration."* Only the delivery adds *"we don't really want that... our goal is to strive
   for some balance"*, which turns it into a concentration risk as well as an asset.
2. **Why hold rate matters mechanically** — *"Meta and TikTok tend to reward creatives that can
   retain users inside the app longer, so... you get better placements and costs."*
3. **What "elevate a proven angle" actually means** — *"more authentic, stronger social proof,
   connect it more with a unique value proposition, agitate the problem more."*

⚠️ **One module has no studio counterpart.** Module 2, Audience Research & Personas, exists only as
the production script. There is no delivered recording of it in the source material, so **the script
and the worksheet are the only record of ACA's persona method.**

⚠️ **A fourth studio recording exists and is an abandoned take.** It opens mid-sentence and breaks
off with Mirella asking *"Should I go back?"*. It was re-shot, and this skill uses the clean version.

## ⚠️ How old this material is

**ACA stamps no recording date on this course.** What can be established:

| | Evidence |
|---|---|
| **Received into this library** | 2026-W31. A "no later than", not a recording date |
| **Internal markers** | The Moments/Signals/Forces framework is credited to TikTok; **GigaBrain**, **aduniverse.ai**, **Answer the Public**, **Foreplay** and **Motion** are named as current tools; the Google Ads Transparency Center is described as *"newer"* |

⛔ **The tool list dates fastest**, and it is load-bearing in two of the four passes. Names,
availability, pricing and access all move. The Google Ads Transparency Center being called "newer"
is a dating marker in itself.

⛔ **Second-fastest is anything about platform behaviour** — the claim that Reels and TikTok
algorithms are discovery-driven, that native aesthetics outperform branded, and that Meta rewards
retention with better placements and costs. All plausible, all unsourced, all subject to change.

⭐ **The method itself dates slowly.** The four passes, the audit structure, copy mining, the
five-dimension competitor framework and the Moments/Signals/Forces hierarchy are not tied to any
product or platform behaviour.

## What is quoted and what is organised

**Everything substantive is Mirella's, quoted directly.** **Quotes are verbatim.** This is spoken delivery, so they carry it as spoken — filler, false starts, mid-sentence restarts and the transcript's own slips. Nothing is smoothed, because a tidied quotation is no longer evidence of what was taught. The artefacts below are preserved where quoted and flagged, not corrected.

**Transcription artefacts in the delivered sessions**, listed so anyone matching this against ACA's
files knows what to expect:

- **A passage in module 1 is unrecoverable**: *"In the Dortmund Bology one of major content
  opportunities."* It sits where the script describes the Quick Performance Analysis as a monthly or
  quarterly health check, so the script fills the gap and nothing is lost.
- **Module 4's delivery opens in Italian**, mid-sentence: *"Ora andiamo a un'altra parte critical di
  il puzzle di research che molte brand completamente miscano."* Mirella is Italian; the script gives
  the same sentence in English.
- Tool and product names are frequently mangled (*"chat tpt, clod"* for ChatGPT and Claude, *"through
  plays"* for ThruPlays). Preserved as rendered wherever they are quoted.
- The written script contains two typos, carried in the source and preserved where quoted:
  *"everygreen"* for evergreen and *"live and fie fast"* for die fast.

**This skill's organisation, not ACA's:** the six-file split, the routing table, framing the four
modules as four sequential "passes", and the decision to surface the script-versus-delivery
disagreements explicitly rather than letting a reader hit one by accident.

**This skill's additions, drawn from the material rather than stated by it:**

- **The reading of the unicorn as two findings at once** — the success formula and the concentration
  risk — and the point that step 7's *"are new creatives approaching unicorn status?"* is where the
  risk half gets answered. Mirella states both halves in different places; joining them is this
  skill's.
- **The note that the 7-day versus 30-day comparison is the cheapest diagnostic in the course**, and
  that it separates a creative problem from a scaling problem.
- **The observation that the North Star check has to come first** because every later step inherits
  a wrong metric.
- **The point that creative lifespan is the number most accounts never calculate**, and that refresh
  cadence is guesswork without it.
- ⭐ **The pre-purchase versus post-purchase distinction** between forums and reviews, and why the
  forum state is the one an ad has to meet.
- **The reading of the Olivia exercise as a repeatable transformation** — each hook keeps the
  customer's own noun phrase and changes only the tense or outcome — and the test that follows from
  it: a phrase appearing nowhere in the research was invented rather than mined.
- **The persona test read as a quality check on the persona itself**, not just on the creative.
- ⛔ **The point that the AI prompts summarise material you supply and do not find it**, which is what
  makes Mirella's manual-first caution operational rather than merely worthy.
- **The framing of run length as a behavioural signal** when it is repetition-with-variation, which
  is the strongest read the Ad Library allows.
- **The note that step 2 of the trends framework is the skipped one**, and that a trend passing none
  of the four relevance tests is a costume.
- **The observation that the seven organic formats double as a coverage checklist** for an existing
  creative mix.
- **The reading of the case study as "invert the emotional payload, keep the structure"**, which is
  the transferable part rather than the 42%.
- **The point that most trend mistakes are a level mismatch** between Moments, Signals and Forces.

## ⚠️ The figures and claims are ACA's

Carried as given, and to be cited as her claims rather than as verified fact. **Every one of these is
reported without a source, and all but one concern an unnamed client.**

| Claim | As given |
|---|---|
| Skincare brand | Adding customers' own phrases (*"doesn't feel greasy"*, *"absorbs quickly"*) lifted conversion rates by **30%** |
| Fitness brand | Addressing price objections in the first 5 seconds **cut those objections in half** and boosted conversion |
| Gardening tools | A question found on Answer the Public became *"their best-performing ad ever"* |
| Beauty brand | Using the native phrase *"holy grail product"* sent engagement *"through the roof"* |
| Beauty brand, trend flip | **42% higher CTR** than previous creative |
| Native versus branded | *"The data shows"* native-looking creative tends to outperform branded. **No data named** |
| Platform mechanics | Meta and TikTok reward retention with better placements and costs |

⚠️ **The 42% is a CTR result**, which is the metric furthest from revenue in this list, and the
course elsewhere warns against judging creative on engagement. Worth holding both.

⛔ **The course names no thresholds for any metric it teaches.** There is no benchmark hook rate or
hold rate anywhere in it: the roadmap step asks you to set targets from your own baseline. **Any
threshold attributed to this course would be invented.**

## Scope

**Not a paid-media audit.** Mirella says so directly: *"these are not ad account audits from the paid
media perspective."* No bidding, budget structure, campaign architecture or account hygiene.

It also stops before creative gets made. Concepts, scripts, storyboards and production are later
courses, and she signposts them at the end of every module.

**Course 701 is named inside module 1** — *"comparative analysis reports that are super easy to set
up in Motion. We have an entire module dedicated on how to use Motion for creative analysis"* — so
the tooling for the audit lives in a different course.
