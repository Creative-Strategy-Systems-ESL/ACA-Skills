# The four platform personalities

> "Every channel has its own style, audience mindset, and creative best practices."

Carlotta gives each platform a character. The label is a memory aid; the useful part is the
creative implication attached to each.

---

## Meta — The Data-Driven Diplomat

> "Sophisticated, versatile, and always equipped with insights."

- **Balances broad reach with precise targeting**
- Massive, diverse audience across Facebook, Instagram and Messenger
- Format variety: images, video, carousels, stories
- ⭐ **Robust analytics and conversion tracking** — *"allowing you to measure impact at every stage
  of the customer journey"*
- *"The diplomat knows how to speak to different audiences"* — adapts messaging across demographics
  while holding brand consistency

Suits both broad awareness and highly targeted conversion.

**What makes it stand out creatively:** the diversity of formats, and the ability to tailor content
per audience and placement *"ensuring your creative always fits the context."*

---

## TikTok — The Trendsetter

⚠️ **ACA's script calls this platform two different things** — the slide list says *"The Show
Stopper"* and the section itself says *"The Trendsetter"*. Same platform, same description. See
`provenance.md`.

> Began as a hub for dance challenges for younger generations, *"but it quickly evolved into a
> global stage for creativity, discovery, and cultural influence that now embraces all age groups."*

- Short-form video plus a powerful algorithm that **rewards originality and authenticity**
- Favours **native, lo-fi and user-generated content**
- Thrives on **immediacy and relevance**
- *"Where brands can shape culture"* — sparking trends, driving engagement, connecting playfully

**What makes it stand out creatively:** it encourages *"unconventional, trend-generating ideas"* and
gives brands room to experiment with native, creator-driven content.

---

## Google — The Interpreter

> "Translating customer intent into opportunities for your brand to provide solutions at exactly the
> right moment."

- **Captures high-intent users via Search and Shopping** — *"exceptionally powerful for
  consideration and conversion stages"*
- ⚠️ **But its reach is not only search.** Display, YouTube and Demand Gen work *"across the entire
  funnel"* — a point people miss when they file Google under "bottom of funnel only"
- Advanced demographic and contextual targeting, reaching people on immediate needs and interests

**What makes it stand out creatively:** dynamically adapting creative assets *"using AI and
automation — to serve the most relevant, visually compelling, and contextually tailored ads at
scale."*

---

## YouTube — The Storyteller

> "While YouTube is part of the Google ecosystem, it deserves its own spotlight."

- **A full spectrum of formats** — from quick Shorts to in-depth product demos and reviews
- Short-form **and** long-form video, so it can drive *"everything from instant awareness to deeper
  consideration and action"*
- Reaches **both active searchers and passive viewers**, at every funnel stage
- Long watch times on traditional video plus high engagement on Shorts make it good for testing
  creative and scaling

**What makes it stand out creatively:** *"unmatched versatility — enabling brands to deliver
everything from snappy, trend-driven content to immersive, story-driven experiences."*

# ⭐ Uber, adapting across six channels

The course's masterclass example. Same brand, same promise, six different executions.

| Channel | What Uber does |
|---|---|
| **Meta** | A mix of static visuals, UGC, and meme-inspired creative. *"Statics remain a winning format here, while UGC adds authenticity"* |
| **TikTok** | Native, creator-driven — quick *"travel hack"* videos showing how easy Uber is on the go. Fun, helpful, trend-driven |
| **Google Search** | Direct and functional. Immediacy and convenience, headlines like *"A Ride When You Need It"*, meeting people at the moment of intent |
| **YouTube** | Cinematic storytelling. The *"On Our Way"* campaign uses emotionally charged long-form video around universal moments |
| **LinkedIn** | A business shift — Uber for Business, employee experience and programme management, aimed at decision-makers. Statics and carousels |
| **Out of home** | Airport billboards with simple evocative visuals: *"You're almost there"* — reaching travellers *"in a moment that matters"* |

> "Uber's approach is a masterclass in cross-channel adaptation: **the core promise is always
> consistent, but the format, tone, and message are tailored for each platform and audience.**"

⚠️ **Note what is being adapted and what is not.** The promise — a ride when you need one — never
changes. What changes is the register: functional on Search, emotional on YouTube, professional on
LinkedIn, situational on a billboard. That is the difference between adaptation and running six
unrelated campaigns.

## Using the personalities well

**They are a starting heuristic, not an assignment.** The funnel-mapping section is explicit that
the best strategies use all relevant platforms across the whole journey rather than parking each
platform at one stage.

⚠️ **These descriptions date.** Platform capabilities, ad products and audience composition all
move, and the personalities were written at one moment. The framing survives; the specifics should
be checked. For platform mechanics rather than personality, ACA's dedicated Meta and TikTok courses
go far deeper.
