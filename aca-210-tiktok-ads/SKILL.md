# Course 210: TikTok Ads Essentials

**Mirella Crespi**, Creative Milkshake. 13m 44s, 1 module — **the shortest course in the
programme**, and much denser than that suggests.

> "If you can master TikTok ads, you'll automatically level-up every other paid-social channel you
> touch. Why? Because TikTok hasn't just influenced social media it has re-wired it."

The argument for spending time here even if TikTok is not the priority: Instagram built Reels,
YouTube launched Shorts, Facebook, Snapchat and Pinterest all now serve vertical sound-on scrollers.
*"They're all morphing into the same thing: infinite discovery feeds where entertainment decides
distribution. TikTok simply got there first — and it still writes the rules the others copy six
months later."*

## What this skill needs from you

**Required: none.** Ask a TikTok question and get an answer.

**Optional, and it makes the answer specific:**

1. **What is being sold**, and to whom
2. **Whether anything is running already**, and what the numbers look like
3. **Budget**, since the course's test plan is built for a small one
4. **What has been tried on other platforms**, especially if creative is being ported across

## ⛔ The boundary, and it matters more here than anywhere else in the programme

**This is a 13-minute course.** It covers what it covers, and TikTok is the platform where a
confident-sounding answer is most likely to be invented, because there is so much folk knowledge
about it in circulation.

**What is in these files came from ACA. Nothing else did.**

If someone asks about current ad specs, auction mechanics, Shop integration, live commerce, spend
minimums, policy, regional availability, or anything with a number that is not in
`reference/metrics-and-sprint.md` — **say it is not covered and point at TikTok's own Ads Manager
documentation and Creative Center.** Do not fill the gap.

⚠️ **This course is explicitly dated.** One of its sections is titled *"How the 2025 Algorithm
Decides Your Fate"*. Tool names, thresholds and algorithm behaviour are all as of that point. See
`reference/provenance.md`.

## The through-line

> **"Ads must entertain first and sell as a natural consequence."**

TikTok is *"an entertainment platform disguised as a social network. People arrive in discovery
mode, sound-on, thumb primed for the next dopamine hit. That means ads can't act like interruptions
— they must behave like the very content the audience came to consume."*

Mirella's test for whether you have got it:

> "The easiest way to spot a rookie TikTok ad? You scroll straight past it, and three swipes later
> you've already forgotten the brand. The easiest way to spot a pro? You're telling a friend about
> the product before the video has even finished."

## How to work through it

⛔ **Set the boundary first.** The section above it exists because this is the course most often applied
where it does not belong, and doing that wastes a budget.

1. **Confirm this is actually TikTok**, per the boundary section above. A resized Meta ad is the failure this
   course is written against.
2. **Give the objectives and their formats** from `reference/the-playbook.md`, so the plan is buildable
   before it is creative.
3. **Mine Audience Insights** with the same file — sub-communities and the language they use. This is the
   input the creative principles need.
4. **Teach the six creative principles** in `reference/creative-principles.md`, including the
   Hook-Key Message-CTA formula.
5. **Add the metrics and their refresh thresholds** from `reference/metrics-and-sprint.md`. ⚠️ Note that the
   CTR band here differs from Courses 402 and 701, and say so rather than presenting one as the number.
6. **Stand up the six-day test plan** in the same file if they have a real budget to spend.
7. **Check they can name one sub-community and the words it uses.** A generic audience answer means step 3
   did not happen.

## Answering well

**Lead with the format argument, not the tactics.** The most common failure the course describes is
treating TikTok as a placement to resize into. ACA's figure: a study of 3,500 ads found that
swapping TV-style spots for TikTok-first videos nearly doubled watch time and lifted purchase intent
37%.

**Sub-culture mining is the distinctive idea here.** Not "target interests" — find the weirdly
specific hashtag communities, watch their content, and take *the language* back into the hook.
That is the part people skip and it is what the platform rewards.

**The thresholds are the useful bit.** Most advice about creative fatigue is vague; this course
gives numbers — CPA up 20% on its week-one average, or CTR down 30%, means rotate. Give those.

**Do not oversell the AI tooling.** Symphony and the rest are described as they were; treat named
features as time-stamped rather than current.
