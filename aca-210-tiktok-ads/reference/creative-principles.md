# The six creative principles

Section 7. ACA attributes these to TikTok's internal *"Creative Bootcamp"*, which *"distilled six
principles from thousands of top ads."*

---

## Principle 1 — TikTok-First

> "Go lo-fi, vertical, human."

- **9 × 16 framing with no UI overlap**
- **Real people over studio actors**
- **Natural lighting over studio light**
- **Trending sound or authentic voice** drives memory

⭐ ACA's figure: *"A/B tests showed TikTok-First assets doubled engagement and conversions."*

## Principle 2 — Trends: Force → Moment → Signal

> "Think of a trend as an iceberg."

| Layer | What it is | Example given |
|---|---|---|
| **Forces** | Big societal shifts | Sustainability, AI |
| **Moments** | Flash-wave memes | The "We do not care" transition |
| **Signals** | Subtle patterns | A popular editing style, a sound effect |

**The process: Identify, Isolate, Iterate.** Scroll the For You Page, scan hashtags, check Creative
Center, pick a Moment, inject brand relevance, then iterate three versions.

⚠️ Moments have the shortest shelf life of the three by definition. A Force is safe to build a
campaign on; a Moment is only usable while it is live, and the example above will be long dead.

## Principle 3 — Structure: the Golden Formula

> "**Hook → Key Message → CTA.** The Golden Formula lives or dies in order."

| Stage | Timing | What goes there |
|---|---|---|
| **Hook** | 0-3 s | Suspense, surprise, or strong emotion. ⭐ Increases watch time **up to 1.7×** |
| **Key Message** | 4-15 s | Product benefit, social proof, or transformation |
| **CTA** | Final 5 s | A direct ask |

⭐ **The statistic worth quoting at anyone who leaves the CTA out:**

> "Ads with a strong CTA drive **208% higher purchase intent** — yet **half of TikTok ads skip
> one.**"

### The three creative structures

> "Three easy creative structures:"

1. **Talent-Engagement Forward** — personality first, product woven through
2. **Problem / Product Forward** — pain point, instant reveal, demo, urgent CTA
3. **No Visible Talent** — lo-fi hands-and-product with voice-over

Useful when a brand has no on-camera talent, or when testing whether a face helps at all.

## Principle 4 — Stimulation

> "Fast cuts, kinetic captions, emoji bursts, camera movement, dramatic zooms — each is a
> micro-reward that keeps the brain's attention system firing."

ACA's supporting claim: *"Neuromarketing data shows these devices spike activity in areas tied to
memory and decision-making."*

## Principle 5 — Sound

> "**Sound on TikTok is half the experience.**"

- **Music** sets mood. *"Faster BPM boosts memorability."*
- **Voice-over** clarifies complex value propositions. *"Use text-to-speech if budget is zero."*
- **ASMR and SFX** parallel the action — *"think can-crack or foundation squish."*

⭐ ACA cites Kantar's 2021 cross-platform study: TikTok *"was the only platform where sound-on ads
lifted both purchase intent and brand favourability."*

⚠️ **Note the tension with other platforms.** ACA's Meta course insists you never assume audio is
on and subtitle everything. Both are right for their platform — TikTok is sound-on by default in a
way feed placements elsewhere are not. Do not carry the Meta rule here, or this rule there.

## Principle 6 — Creative Refresh

> "**Even perfect creative dies.**"

The triggers are numeric, which is what makes them usable:

> "Track CPA and CTR. When **CPA rises 20% above its week-one average** or **CTR drops 30%**,
> rotate in fresh assets."

Sources of fresh ideas named in the course: Symphony's *Daily Video Generations* and Creative
Center's *Top Ads Spotlight*.

# Production best practices

Section 10 of the script. **Five practices** — note that "10" is the section number, not a count.

1. **Show one product**; max three if they belong together.
2. **Disclose price or promo early** — transparency drives trust.
3. **Showcase diversity** — let your consumer see themselves.
4. **Sweet-spot length: 20-35 seconds.** Under 15s is for teaser hype; over 40s drains attention.
5. **Keep text and key elements in the safe zone** — the top 20% and bottom 20% are UI land.

⚠️ **Point 4 sits oddly beside the hook guidance and both are in the course.** The Golden Formula
puts the CTA in the final five seconds of a 20-35 second ad, so the two are consistent — but if
someone has absorbed "keep it under six seconds" from a Meta context, flag the difference rather
than blending them. This platform tolerates, and often wants, longer.

⚠️ **Safe-zone percentages and specs date.** Check TikTok's current ad specs before building to
them.
