# How the algorithm decides

⚠️ **The course's own heading is "How the 2025 Algorithm Decides Your Fate."** Treat everything in
this section as describing that point in time.

Three pillars the ranking model feeds on:

1. **User interactions** — swipes, rewatches, comments, shares, profile taps
2. **Video information** — captions, on-screen text, hashtags, sounds
3. **Device and account settings** — language, locale, device type

## ⭐ But the power curve is the first three seconds

> "Your opening frame drives the earliest watch-time signal, and **that signal tells the system
> whether or not to push your video to the next thousand people.**"

Two consequences the course draws:

- **Hook visually** — a reveal, a shock, a question — *"before the viewer even decides if they'll
  keep the sound on."* The visual has to work before the audio decision is made.
- **Speak to a niche, not the masses.** *"The algorithm now shows preference for sub-communities
  like #BookTok, #CarTok, #STEMTok. When the niche explodes, you ride the wave organically."*

# The six metrics that matter

> "**Vanity metrics make you feel busy; algorithmic metrics make you rich.**"

| Metric | Healthy | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **3-second hold rate** | **≥ 70%** | *"If lower, your hook is ice-cold"* |
| **Average watch time** | **1.4× video length** | Rewatches trump single passes |
| **Completion rate** | **18-25%** for conversion ads | Higher for storytelling |
| **Engagement rate** | **4-8%** for consumer brands | (likes + comments + shares) ÷ impressions |
| **Click-through rate** | **> 2%** for ecommerce | Below it, *"your CTA or landing page mismatches the promise"* |
| **ROAS / CPA trend line** | — | ⚠️ **A 20% week-over-week CPA rise is the canary for creative fatigue.** Kill or refresh |

⚠️ **These thresholds are ACA's, given without a source or a vertical.** They are usable as
starting reference points and should be replaced by the account's own baselines once it has them.
Do not present them to a client as industry benchmarks.

⛔ **Two of them collide with ACA's own Course 402, and neither course mentions the other.**

- **CTR.** This course puts the ecommerce floor at **above 2%**. Course 402 puts the sweet spot at
  **0.9% to 1.3%** and treats anything over 1.4% as a reason to check whether the traffic is
  qualified. An ad at 1.1% is healthy by one course and broken by the other. **They are different
  platforms** — this is TikTok, 402 measures Meta link clicks out to a store — **and near-certainly
  different CTR definitions**, since 402 establishes that Ads Manager holds five distinct CTRs and
  this course names no variant.
- **The 3-second hold rate.** This course gives no formula for it. If it means 3-second views ÷
  impressions, then a 70% floor cannot be squared with 402's *"50% or higher is very rare"*. If it
  means something else, the two numbers are not about the same thing.

⭐ **So: never carry a threshold from this table onto another platform, and say which CTR you mean.**
`aca-402-key-metrics` has the formulas and the full comparison.

# Creative at scale: the tooling

Section 4. ⚠️ **Named product features, and the most time-bound content in the course.** Given as
described; verify anything before relying on it.

TikTok One and **Symphony**:

- **Generate & Remix** — paste a product page URL, receive five ready-to-run videos in minutes
- **Avatar Videos** — a multilingual host lip-syncing your script. ACA's cited result: *"Vodafone
  slashed cost-per-lead 45%"*
- **Translate & Dub** — one click, thirty languages, lip alignment
- **Daily Video Generations** — Symphony watches yesterday's winners and proposes fresh spins

Also named as *"your cheapest and fastest staff creators and editors"*: **CapCut templates**, the
in-app Video Editor, and Symphony's Daily Generations.

## ⭐ The minimum viable habit

> "Keep **five to seven unique creatives live in every conversion campaign** and **refresh them
> weekly.** That single habit is the cheapest antidote to creative fatigue."

⚠️ **Weekly is a TikTok rhythm.** ACA's Meta course puts fatigue at *"week four, week six, or week
eight"*. Both are their platform's own guidance — TikTok's feed turns over faster — so do not apply
a weekly refresh budget to a Meta account, or a six-week one here.

# The creative iteration loop

Section 11. *"TikTok rewards volume and variation."*

**Structure:** 3 to 5 creatives per ad group, multiple ad groups per campaign.

|---|---|
| **1** | Launch varied hooks |

# The six-day action plan

Section 12. The runnable part of the course, built for a small budget.

> "how to use TikTok's tools and ecosystem to quickly test and and unlock winning concepts in only 1
> week without much budget and effort."

| Day | Action |
|---|---|
| **1** | Pull Audience Insights; **list three sub-cultures, write three scripts** |
| **2** | Spin five variants in Symphony + CapCut; **tag each with the principle it exemplifies** |
| **3** | Launch a test budget — **$50 per ad set, auto-placement** ⚠️ see note |
| **4** | Check 3-second hold rate and CTR; **kill the bottom two** |
| **5** | Remix the best performer twice; **change hook or sound only** |
| **6** | Scale top ads, archive losers, document learnings, repeat |

⭐ **Day 2's tagging instruction is the part that makes the week worth repeating.** Tagging each
variant with the principle it demonstrates means day 4's kill decision teaches you which *principle*
worked, not merely which file won.

⚠️ **And it is what keeps day 2 from contradicting ACA's Meta course.** Course 201's Andromeda module
is blunt that *"more ads is not automatically a strategy"* and that five cosmetic variants of one
idea give the system nothing to work with. Day 2 asks for five variants **before** anything is
proven — which looks like exactly that.

**The reconciliation is the tag.** Five variants each demonstrating a *different principle* are five
distinct creative inputs, which is what both courses actually ask for. Five variants that differ
only in colour are the thing 201 warns against, on either platform. **Spin five, but make them five
different arguments.**

⚠️ **Day 5 changes one layer only** — hook or sound — which keeps the test readable. Changing the
concept and the hook together tells you nothing about either.

⚠️ **The $50 per ad set is ACA's illustrative figure**, not a platform minimum. Check TikTok's
current minimums, and scale the number to the account.

### ⚠️ Auto-placement: this course says yes, ACA's Meta course says no

Day 3 runs on auto-placement, and the campaign-architecture section says **95% of advertisers can
leave placements automatic** because *"TikTok's auction is placement-agnostic."*

ACA's Course 201 says the opposite for Meta: ⛔ *"do not lean on auto-placement... often the resized
ads don't look their best. Not really great at all."*

⭐ **Both are right, for different reasons, and the reason is the point.** Meta's auto-placement
**resizes one asset into placements it was not built for**, which is why it degrades. TikTok's
placement choice is about *where the auction serves it*, not about reformatting your creative — and
TikTok is one dominant vertical format rather than a spread of shapes.

**So: automatic on TikTok, per-placement builds on Meta.** Do not carry either rule across.
