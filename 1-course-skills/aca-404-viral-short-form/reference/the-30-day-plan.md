# The 30-day plan

ACA ships this as a **six-sheet working planner**: Start Here · Week 01 · Week 02 · Week 03 ·
Week 04 · 30-Day Summary. Luke's own subtitle on it is *"print + pin to wall"*.

⛔ **Emit these sheets with their real fields.** Markdown tables or CSV, one block per sheet, so the
output pastes back into the workbook. A prose plan is not the deliverable.

⭐ **Each pillar is labelled with the metric it is supposed to move**, which is the sheet's best
idea. Educational earns saves, entertainment earns shares, behind-the-scenes earns follows. A channel
underperforming on one metric usually has an underfed pillar.

⚠️ **These three pillars are Luke's and are for organic content.** ACA's Course 302 separately
mentions "the 3 pillars — Educational, Entertaining, Aspirational" which it never defines. Different
authors, different jobs. **Do not merge them.**

## The launch sequence, from the course

Module 5 gives the day-by-day that the planner assumes:

| When | What |
|---|---|
| **Days 1-3** | Finalise the filming space and gear. Work out which of the five roles need filling, and who fills them |
| **Days 4-5** | Build a two-week calendar using the 60/30/10 rule and your three pillars |
| **Days 6-7** | Script the first batch. Schedule a brainstorm session |
| **Then** | Film in one batch session, edit, start posting on cadence. **Set up the accountability system** |
| **Week 3** | Introduce the first experimental format. ⭐ *"be on social media for at least 15 to 30 minutes a day to see what's successful online. Copy the links of all the videos you like, put them in the notes app"* |
| | Then run the first weekly content review |
| **Week 4** | Optimise. Look back over four weeks, refine hooks, formats, process |

**By the end of week four**, per the course: a dialled-in workflow · **15 to 20+ published pieces** ·
clear data · month two's calendar already sketched.

> "Perfection is the enemy of actually publishing. Get out there and do it. Press record."

## Producing a filled plan

Deliver, in order:

1. **Start Here** — the three pillars with five ideas each, the cadence commitment, the goals
2. **Week 01** at minimum, with the mix targets and the 7-day calendar filled
3. **Weeks 02 to 04** as scaffolding if the concepts do not exist yet, with the mix targets set
4. **30-Day Summary** left empty, because it is filled at the end
5. ⛔ **An open-fields line** — what was not supplied and therefore not filled

⛔ **Do not invent the goals.** View and follower targets are the client's commitment, not yours, and
a number you supply becomes one they are measured against. Ask, or leave `⚠️ TO SET`.

⚠️ **Fifteen content ideas is the ask on Start Here**, five per pillar, and it is the hardest part of
the sheet. If the brand's material only supports eight, say so and name which pillar is thin. **A
thin pillar is a finding**, not something to pad.

**Offer CSV if it is going into the workbook**, one block per sheet, headers in row one.
