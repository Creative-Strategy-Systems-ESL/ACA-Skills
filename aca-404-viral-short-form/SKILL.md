# Course 404: Engineering Viral Short-Form Content

**Luke Kostka**, new norml media. 37m 30s, 6 modules plus an intro.

He states his own numbers up front: **2.5 billion views, 7,000+ videos, 2 million followers**, built
from a garage through Alex, AD World and ClickUp.

> "This course is built on three pillars: **resources, experimentation, and consistency.**"

⭐ **This skill emits the 30-day plan.** ACA ships a six-sheet working planner and the job here is to
fill it, in its real columns, so it pastes back into their workbook.

## ⚠️ Read this before applying any of it to ads

**This is an organic content course, not a paid one.** Every framework in it is tuned for a feed
that rewards watch time and follows, and the whole system is built on posting volume nobody buys
media against.

⛔ **The 60/30/10 mix, the cadence ladder and the 90-day commitment do not transfer to paid.** A paid
account testing 60 pieces of creative a quarter is a different budget conversation entirely. What
does transfer is the hook work, the pattern interrupts and the story frameworks.

**Say which you are giving.** Someone arriving from a paid-social problem needs to know this course
answers a different question.

## What this skill needs from you

**Required: none, to explain any of it.**

**To produce a filled 30-day plan, required:**

1. **The brand or person**, and what they actually do
2. **Who it is for** — Luke's own turning point was learning ClickUp's audience were project
   managers
3. **Realistic capacity** — who films, who edits, how many hours a week

**Optional, and each fills real fields:**

4. Primary and secondary platforms
5. Existing content, and how it has performed
6. Whether anyone is on camera, and who
7. Who the accountability partner will be

⛔ **Capacity is the input people overstate, and the plan is worthless if it is wrong.** A cadence
picked optimistically fails in week three and takes the 90-day commitment down with it. Ask what
they can sustain on a bad week, not a good one.

## How to run it

⚠️ **Read the boundary at the top first.** This is organic short-form, and applying it to paid ads unchanged
is the failure the opening warning exists to prevent.

1. **Confirm organic, not paid.** If they want ads, this is the wrong course.
2. **Set up the three pillars** from `reference/the-three-pillars.md` — resources, experimentation,
   consistency — and name the five roles even if one person wears all of them.
3. **Fix the content mix** at 60/30/10 and the posting cadence, same file. ⚠️ Cadence is a 90-day commitment,
   not a preference.
4. **Teach the hook and story frameworks** in `reference/hooks-and-stories.md`, including the three kinds of
   pattern interrupt.
5. ⭐ **Produce the 30-day plan** from `reference/the-30-day-plan.md` — Start Here, the four week sheets, and
   the 30-Day Summary. All six sheets.
6. **Install the weekly review**, thirty minutes, from the same file. Without it the plan is a calendar.
7. **Check they have committed to a cadence they can actually hold.** An ambitious cadence abandoned in week
   three is worse than a modest one kept.

## Answering well

**Equipment is not the blocker and saying so is the useful part.**

> "I've seen brands spend ten K on a studio and post twice a month. And on the other hand, some of
> them and brands film in a closet with a ring light post daily and get millions of views.
> **Equipment is a tool, not**"

**Volume is the mechanism, not the goal.** One video a week versus five is 12 posts against 60 over
90 days: *"five times more data, five times more shots on goal."* The argument is for learning rate,
not for spam.

**Safe content is the actual risk.** *"Safe content, polished brand videos, predictable tips, they
all flatline. Bold content spikes. Sometimes it fails. When it hits, it explodes."*

⛔ **Never promise what the body does not deliver.** *"Clickbait with a weak payoff kills trust."*
This is the one rule that constrains all the hook work.

⚠️ **Every figure in this course is Luke's and unsourced.** Completion rates, the 44-second stat, the
100-post threshold. `reference/provenance.md` lists them. Quote them as his claims.
