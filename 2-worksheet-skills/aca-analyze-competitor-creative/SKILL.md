# Analyse competitor creative

**Ad Creative Academy**, Course 302 (Mirella Crespi), module 3.

> "We're not looking at competitors to copy them. We're investigating to get inspired and find gaps
> in the market that we can own. **Big difference.**"

## What this skill needs from you

**Required:**

1. **The brand** this analysis is for, and what it sells
2. **A competitor set**, or enough about the brand to build one. ACA's own guidance is 5 to 6
3. **The ads**, or access to them. Public ad libraries are free and are where this runs

**Optional, and each sharpens it:**

4. Your own brand's current creative, so the output is a comparison rather than a description
5. Which platforms matter, if not all of them
6. Whether this is for internal use or a client presentation, which decides whether slide 5 matters
7. Any known positioning or price differences across the set

⚠️ **Analysis without seeing the ads is not analysis.** If no ads or library links are supplied, say
which libraries to pull from (Meta Ad Library, TikTok Creative Center, Google Ads Transparency
Center) and what to capture. A worksheet filled from general knowledge of a brand is a description of
its reputation, not of its creative.

## ⛔ The rule that governs every reading here

> "**Just because an ad is running doesn't mean it's working.** I can't tell you how many times I've
> seen brands dump money into campaigns that are absolute duds simply because they had the budget to
> keep them going."

> "You never know what their conversion objective is or what their CPA goal is."

**Everything visible from outside is a signal about budget and attention, never about
profitability.** You cannot see their target, their margin, or whether anyone is watching the
account.

⛔ **So every inference in the output has to be labelled as one.** "They are testing heavily" is
observable. "This is working for them" is not, and stating it is the single most common way this
analysis misleads the person who commissioned it.

## How to run it

1. **One worksheet per competitor.** `reference/the-worksheet.md`. Do not merge them.
2. **Work the sections in order** — overview, volume and budget, format, execution, temporal.
3. **Then step back across the set**, which is where the actual value is. A single competitor
   worksheet tells you little; the pattern across five tells you what the category believes.
4. **Turn it into position** using the five strategic moves.
5. **Build the deck** if it is going to a client. `reference/the-presentation.md`.

## Answering well

**The gap is the deliverable, not the inventory.** Five filled worksheets are input. The output is
white space: *"angles, hooks, or claims that nobody in your space is making."*

**Read timing, not just creative.** Ads launched the same day are a test cohort. An ad that has run
for months is a candidate winner, subject to the warning above. This is the closest thing to
performance data available from outside.

⭐ **Repetition with variation is the strongest signal you can see.** *"If you see a competitor using
the same hook structure but with different visuals or products, they've probably found a winner
they're scaling."* It is behavioural: it is what a brand does when it has something it does not want
to lose. Rate it above raw run length.

**Elevate rather than copy.** *"When you see an angle that's clearly working, don't just copy it —
elevate it."* Operationally, and in Mirella's own words: *"make it more authentic, add stronger
social proof, connect it more with a unique value proposition, agitate the problem more."*

⚠️ **Engagement is not conversion.** TikTok Creative Center's Top Ads are *"often sorted by
engagement, not by actual business results."* And: *"when an ad has tons of likes but few substantive
comments, be skeptical. But when you see comments like 'Just ordered!' or 'This solved my exact
problem!' — that's a much stronger signal."*

**Set a cadence.** *"Spend an hour every month refreshing your competitive analysis."* An hour a
month is the whole ask, and it is worth saying to anyone treating this as a quarterly project they
never start.

## ⚠️ This is not Course 301's job

**301 reverse-engineers one winning ad into a reusable structure. This maps the landscape.** If the
question is "how do I make my own version of this specific ad", that is the other course. If it is
"who are we up against and where is the gap", it is this one.
