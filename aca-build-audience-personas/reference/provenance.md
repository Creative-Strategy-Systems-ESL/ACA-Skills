# Provenance

## Source

**Ad Creative Academy** — three assets shipped with **Course 302: Research for Creative Strategy**,
module 2, taught by **Mirella Crespi** of Creative Milkshake, ACA's founder:

1. **Audience Research and Persona Development Worksheet** — 26 pages, 6 parts
2. **Audience Persona Slide Template** — the two-column presentation artifact
3. **AI Prompts and Tools for Automating Audience Research** — 5 parts, 12 prompts, plus tool
   recommendations

## ⚠️ This module has no delivered recording

Every other module of Course 302 exists twice, as a written production script and as a delivered
studio session. **Module 2 exists only as the script.** There is no studio counterpart anywhere in
the source material.

⛔ **So the script and these worksheets are the only record of ACA's persona method.** Where the other
modules' skills can cross-check a script against a delivery and surface disagreements, this one
cannot. If something here is ambiguous, there is no second source to settle it.

## ⚠️ Three naming quirks in ACA's own materials

1. **The slide template is titled "Audience Person Slide Template"** on the artifact itself — Person,
   not Persona. A typo in ACA's own title, carried here so a search for either finds it.
2. **The prompt pack is tracked under three names.** Its own title is *"AI Prompts and Tools for
   Automating Audience Research"*; Mirella calls it her *"AI Research Accelerator"* on the recording;
   and ACA's tracked-download list carries *"Complete Research Workflow"* as a separate row, which is
   **Part 5 of the same PDF**. One document, three rows.
3. **The PDFs stamp "Course 301"** in their footers. ACA's shipping curriculum numbers this course
   **302**; 301 is Zach Murray's Reverse Engineering Winning Ads. ACA renumbered for launch and the
   worksheets were stamped before that. This skill uses 302.

## What is reproduced and what is this skill's

**Reproduced as written:** the worksheet's six parts and every capture table, the slide template's
two columns and six rows with ACA's filled example in full, the four research questions, the persona
fields, the Olivia worked example with all three phrase-to-hook pairs, and the prompt pack's five
parts with its named tools.

**This skill's, drawn from the material rather than stated by it:**

- ⛔ **The refusal to generate personas with no raw material**, and the labelled-hypothesis route when
  a request is urgent. ACA does not address what to do when there is nothing to mine.
- ⭐ **Naming the Creative Translation column as the point of the artifact**, and the rule that a
  persona with that column empty is a description rather than a tool. ACA presents the two columns as
  equals.
- ⭐ **The reading of the Olivia exercise as a repeatable transformation** — each hook keeps the
  customer's own noun phrase and changes only the tense or outcome, introducing nothing new — and the
  test that follows: **a phrase appearing nowhere in the research was invented, not mined.** Mirella
  says *"we're not inventing messaging"*; the mechanical rule and the test are this skill's.
- ⭐ **The pre-purchase versus post-purchase distinction** between forums and reviews, and the
  consequence: mine only reviews and your hooks speak to people who have already bought.
- ⚠️ **The sampling caution.** Reviewers skew delighted or furious; forum posters skew engaged or
  frustrated; the quiet middle is invisible in both. ACA does not raise this anywhere.
- **The instruction to keep verbatims ugly**, because cleaning up grammar is how mined language turns
  back into marketing English.
- **The reading of the persona test as a quality check on the persona**, not only on the creative.
- **The note that part 2's Language Collection is deliberately upstream of persona building**, so
  clusters emerge from the language rather than the language being sorted into predetermined
  personas.
- **The three checks on the prompt pack's output**: traceability to quotes, smoothed language, and
  uncounted frequency claims.
- **The guidance on how many personas**, which ACA does not give: seven usually means no clustering
  happened, one usually means the research was too narrow.
- **The instruction to mark inferred rows inside the cell**, because personas get pasted into decks.

## ⚠️ The figures and claims are ACA's

Mirella reports four client results in this module. **Every one concerns an unnamed client and none
is sourced.** Carry them as her claims.

| Claim | As given |
|---|---|
| Skincare brand | Adding customers' own phrases (*"doesn't feel greasy"*, *"absorbs quickly"*) lifted conversion rates by **30%** |
| Fitness brand | Addressing the price objection in the first 5 seconds **cut those objections in half** and boosted conversion |
| Gardening tools | A question found on Answer the Public (*"Why do my plants keep dying even when I water them?"*) became *"their best-performing ad ever"* |
| Beauty brand | Using the native phrase *"holy grail product"* sent engagement *"through the roof"* |

⚠️ **All four share a shape**: a phrase was mined, inserted, and performance improved. That is the
module's argument, and these are the evidence for it. **They are anecdotes from the person teaching
the method**, which does not make them false and does mean they should not be quoted as data.

## ⚠️ How old this material is

ACA stamps no date. Received into this library in **2026-W31**, a "no later than" rather than a
publication date.

⛔ **The tool lists date fastest**, and there are four of them. **GigaBrain, Reviewbox, Bazaarvoice,
MonkeyLearn, Brandwatch, BuzzSumo, SEMrush, SparkToro, People Data Labs, Dovetail** and **Answer the
Public** are named as current. Availability, pricing and access all move, and several are small
enough to disappear. Check before recommending any of them.

⭐ **The method dates slowly.** Copy mining, the four questions, the persona structure and the
two-column translation do not depend on any tool. Where a named tool is gone, the source type
(reviews, forums, ad comments, search questions) still exists.

## Deliberate overlap with two other skills

Each must run with nothing else installed.

- **`aca-302-research-for-creative-strategy`** teaches all four research passes and explains why
  audience research sits second. It does not produce the artifacts.
- **This skill** runs pass 2 for a real brand and produces the personas.
- **ACA's Course 102 has its own consumer-psychology worksheet**, which is a different job: awareness
  stages, persuasion principles and message framing, working from theory toward a message. This one
  works from the audience's recorded words toward a persona. **Route on whether there is raw customer
  language to mine.**
