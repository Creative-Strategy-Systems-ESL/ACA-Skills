# Run the Creative QA Checklist

Produces **Ad Creative Academy's Course 501 artifact** — Mirella Crespi's eight-page Creative QA
Checklist, filled.

> "Use this checklist to ensure quality and performance optimization for all creative assets
> **before launching them in your ad accounts.**"

⭐ **ACA states two purposes, and the second is the one people miss:**

> "This tool serves two purposes: **catching technical errors** and **optimizing for performance.**
> The performance sections are particularly valuable for **training team members to develop their
> creative intuition.**"

⚠️ **So a filled checklist is a teaching document as well as a gate.** Write the "needs attention"
notes so somebody learns from them, not just so the row is filled.

## What this skill needs from you

**Required:**

1. **The asset**, or a detailed enough description to judge it — what happens on screen and when,
   what is said, what is on screen as text
2. **The platform and placement** it is going to run on. Half the technical rows cannot be scored
   without it

**Optional, and each one turns rows from unscoreable into scoreable:**

3. The storyboard or brief it was made from. ⛔ Without it, the whole Creative Integrity section is
   unscoreable — it asks whether anything was missed
4. Brand guidelines: colours, logo, fonts, required disclaimers
5. The other hooks in the batch, for the Hook Variation section
6. The conversion objective, for the CTA rows
7. Whether it is a branded, native or expert ad

⛔ **Never mark a row from a description that could not settle it.** *"Clean audio with no background
noise"* cannot be judged from a script. **An unscoreable row is marked `Cannot assess`**, with what
would be needed. A checklist of confident ticks that nobody actually verified is worse than no
checklist, because it certifies the ad.

⛔ **Never invent a brand guideline.** If the brand's fonts, colours and disclaimers were not
supplied, the Brand Compliance rows are `Cannot assess` — not a pass.

## How to run it

**Both halves, in order.** Technical Review first, because a failed spec makes the performance
questions academic.

1. ⛔ **Confirm the asset is actually here.** This skill cannot run on a description of an ad; it needs the
   file or a frame-by-frame account of it.
2. **Establish the placement and platform**, because the spec rows are judged against them.
3. **Run the Technical Review** from **ACA's Creative QA Checklist** (below), in the order the checklist gives.
4. **Then the performance half**, same file, including the runtime bands and the heartbeat method.
5. **Mark every row** with one of the four marks below. ⛔ Leaving a row blank reads as a pass.
6. **Emit the verdict from coverage, not just failures.** ⚠️ A checklist with six *Cannot assess* rows and no
   failures is not a pass — say what could not be judged and what is needed to judge it.
7. **Name who else should review it**, per ACA's two process rules below.

**Every row gets one of four marks:**

| Mark | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Good to Go** | Checked and passes |
| **Needs attention** | Checked and fails, with what to change |
| **Cannot assess** | ⛔ Not judgeable from what was supplied, with what is missing |
| **N/A** | Genuinely does not apply, with why in the cell |

⭐ **ACA's own two process rules, and they pull against each other on purpose:**

> "**Have at least two team members review each asset** but keep the QA process efficient and
> focused."

> "The QA process should be **thorough but not endless.** Establish clear guidelines and timeframes
> to prevent perfectionism from delaying your testing and learning cycles."

⚠️ **This skill is one set of eyes, not two.** Say so when handing the filled checklist over: ACA
asks for a second human reviewer, and an automated pass does not satisfy that.

## ⚠️ What this skill is not

**It is not a performance prediction.** Every row is a craft check on the asset in front of you.
Whether it works is decided by the numbers after it runs, which is `aca-402-key-metrics`.

**It is not a strategy review.** Whether the concept was right is Course 401's question, and whether
the storyboard was right is Course 403's.

**It is not a legal or compliance sign-off.** It flags missing disclaimers and copyright risk as
craft items; it does not replace whoever actually approves claims.

## ACA's Creative QA Checklist

⛔ **Emit every row below, marked.** Not a summary of what was checked.

**Two halves, and a separate pass for image ads.** Every row is scored **Good to Go** or **Needs
attention** in ACA's original, plus `Cannot assess` and `N/A` for the honest cases ACA does not
provide for.

---

## Part 1: Technical Review

### Format & Specifications

| Row |
|---|
| **Asset length appropriate for platform and concept** |
| **Correct aspect ratios delivered for each platform** |
| **Safe zones respected** — no key elements in areas covered by UI elements |

#### ⭐ ACA's runtime bands

| Platform | Length |
|---|---|
| **TikTok** | **7-40 seconds** |
| **Meta** | **15-45 seconds** |
| **YouTube** | **20-45 seconds** |

> "**Only VSL ads can be longer than 1 minute.**"

#### The aspect ratios

| Platform | Ratios |
|---|---|
| **Meta** | 9:16, 4:5 and 1:1 |
| **TikTok** | 9:16 |
| **YouTube** | 9:16 and 16:9 |

⛔ **These are ACA's numbers, current as of an undated recording.** Platform specs change. Score
against them, and say in the output that the current platform documentation should be the final
word before delivery.

### Text and Audio Check

| Row |
|---|
| **Zero spelling / grammar errors** |
| **Correct fonts** — brand fonts for branded ads, native fonts for UGC/native formats — **and sized for mobile legibility** |
| **Supers support rather than distract from audio** |
| **Closed captions match spoken words and never cover key visuals** |
| **Clean audio** with no background noise or static |
| **Consistent volume levels throughout** |
| **Music/SFX balanced properly with voice** |
| **Does the music add to the ad, or is it distracting?** |
| ⛔ **No copyright issues with music or sound effects** |
| ⭐ **Does the hook have an audio pattern interrupt / "ear stopper" in the first 3 seconds?** |

⚠️ **The captions row is two tests in one** — accuracy *and* placement. A caption that is word-perfect
and sits over the product fails it.

### Brand Compliance

| Row |
|---|
| **Brand colours, logo, disclaimers used exactly to guidelines** |
| **Tone of voice and visuals matches brand** |
| **Disclaimers included when required** |

⛔ **All three are `Cannot assess` without the brand guidelines.** "Looks on-brand to me" is not a
check against a guideline.

### Creative Integrity Check

| Row |
|---|
| ⭐ **Has anything been missed from the storyboards?** |
| **If there are before and afters, are they clear? Do we CLEARLY see a before and after?** |
| **Does the ad feel authentic and relatable, or does it feel fake?** |
| **Have we added strong testimonials and social proof?** |
| **If the ad is a street interview, is the acting natural?** |
| **If it's a podcast, does the setup and lighting look good / professional / legit?** |
| **Do the screen recordings highlight the key feature, not generic scrolling?** |
| **Props, lighting and setting match the narrative** — e.g. night scenes for sleep products |

⛔ **The first row needs the storyboard.** Without it this whole section is `Cannot assess`, and it
is the section that catches the most expensive class of error: the shot that was briefed, paid for
and never delivered.

⚠️ **Three of these rows are format-conditional** — before/afters, street interview, podcast. Mark
them `N/A` with the reason rather than passing them by default.

## Part 2: Performance Optimization

### Hook Effectiveness (first 3 seconds)

| Row |
|---|
| **Pattern interrupt present?** Movement, strong visual |
| **Does the hook create curiosity or emotion?** |
| **Clear persona represented?** |
| **Problem statement immediate and clear?** |
| **Engaging visual that supports the message?** |
| **Does the sound work as an "ear-stopper"?** |

⭐ **These six are the five elements of a hook, plus the pattern interrupt.** An ad failing three of
them does not need a note on each; it needs a new opener.

### Hook Variation

| Row |
|---|
| **Are the hooks sufficiently different from each other?** |
| **Does each hook test a different visual approach or messaging angle?** |

⛔ **This section needs the rest of the batch.** One asset in isolation cannot be scored on variation
— mark it `Cannot assess` and ask for the siblings.

### Thumbnail Assessment

| Row |
|---|
| **Is the first frame strong?** Is there enough on frame to make the person stop scrolling? |
| **Is the visual interesting? Is there a strong headline?** Combined, what is their effect? |
| **Product or key visual clearly visible?** |
| **Would this stand out in a crowded feed?** |

### Pacing and Structure

| Row |
|---|
| **Quick pacing with no dead spots** |
| ⭐ **Did we use the heartbeat method? Something changes every 1.5 seconds** |
| **Clear story progression** — Hook → Body → CTA |
| **Effective use of pattern interrupts throughout the whole ad** |
| **No unnecessary pauses or filler content** — no longer than 2-3 seconds on the same visual |

⭐ **The heartbeat method is the most checkable row in the whole document**, and it appears nowhere in
the course narration. A change every 1.5 seconds is countable from a timeline, so score it with a
number rather than an impression.

### Call to Action

| Row |
|---|
| **Clear, specific CTA present** |
| **CTA aligns with conversion objective** |
| **Urgency created when appropriate** |
| **Visually distinct from other content** |
| **Reinforces main value proposition** |

⚠️ **Row 2 needs the objective.** A CTA that says "learn more" is right for traffic and wrong for
purchase, and nothing on the asset itself reveals which was bought.

### Final Gut Check

| Row |
|---|
| **Would you stop and watch it end-to-end?** |
| **Do these look good to you? (Be honest)** |
| **Do these ads follow the brief?** |
| **Are you proud to send these for client review?** |

⭐ **ACA put "(Be honest)" in the document itself**, which tells you what usually happens to this
section. It is the only part of the checklist that cannot be gamed by a spec sheet, and a batch that
passes every technical row and fails the gut check is a batch worth stopping.

## The image-ad pass

⚠️ **A separate page in ACA's checklist**, for statics rather than video.

| Row |
|---|
| **Where does your eye go first? Is that the intended effect?** ⭐ *Look at the asset on your phone* |
| **Is it easy to read? Can you cut out any words from the copy?** |
| **Are you using the right visual? Does it compete too much with the messaging?** |
| **Take a look at your inspiration ad** — the reference in the brief. Is it better or worse? |
| **Is the copy strong? Is it VERY clear what problem this product or service solves?** |
| ⛔ **Is this a Branded, Native or Expert ad? Is that CLEAR? It should not be a mix of these** |
| **How can you make your ad better?** |
| **Do these look good to you? (Be honest)** |
| **Do these ads follow the brief?** |
| **Are you proud to send these for client review?** |

⭐ **"Look at the asset on your phone" is a literal instruction and it changes results.** Everything
here is judged at feed size on a handset, not at 100% on a monitor.

⛔ **The Branded / Native / Expert row is the one with teeth.** A static that is half native and half
branded reads as neither, and this is the only row in the whole document that names a failure mode
rather than a quality.

## Producing the filled checklist

**Emit it in this shape**, one row per checklist item:

```csv
Part,Section,Row,Mark,Note
```

⚠️ **Illustrative rows, to show the marking.** Every real row gets its own line.

| Part | Section | Row | Mark | Note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Technical | Format & Specifications | Asset length appropriate | **Needs attention** | 52s against ACA's 15-45s Meta band, and not a VSL. Cut to under 45s |
| Technical | Format & Specifications | Correct aspect ratios | **Needs attention** | 9:16 only. Meta also needs 4:5 and 1:1 |
| Technical | Brand Compliance | Brand colours, logo, disclaimers | ⛔ **Cannot assess** | No brand guidelines supplied |
| Technical | Creative Integrity | Anything missed from the storyboards? | ⛔ **Cannot assess** | Storyboard not supplied |
| Technical | Creative Integrity | Street interview acting natural? | **N/A** | Not a street interview |
| Performance | Hook Effectiveness | Ear-stopper in first 3 seconds | **Needs attention** | Opens on music only. Add a spoken line or a sound effect on the first beat |
| Performance | Pacing | Heartbeat method, change every 1.5s | **Good to Go** | 14 changes in 22s, average 1.6s, longest hold 2.4s |
| Performance | Hook Variation | Hooks sufficiently different? | ⛔ **Cannot assess** | Only one asset supplied. Send the batch |
| Performance | Final Gut Check | Proud to send for client review? | **Needs attention** | Not until the ratios and the runtime are fixed |

### ⛔ Before delivering it

1. **A count at the top**: how many Good to Go, Needs attention, Cannot assess, N/A
2. ⛔ **The `Cannot assess` rows listed together with what would settle them**, because that list is
   the actual request back to whoever sent the asset
3. **A launch verdict in one line** — and it is `hold` if any Technical row failed, whatever the
   performance half says
   - ⛔ **It is also `hold` if any Technical row is `Cannot assess`.** The verdict keys on coverage,
     not only on failures: a checklist where nothing failed *because nothing could be checked* is
     exactly the certification `Cannot assess` exists to prevent. **Never clear an asset for launch
     off a pass you could not perform** — the honest verdict is `hold, pending <the missing inputs>`
4. ⚠️ **The reminder that ACA asks for at least two human reviewers**, and this pass is one set of
   eyes
5. ⛔ **No row silently dropped.** Every row in the document appears in the output with a mark

⚠️ **Do not soften a Needs attention into a suggestion.** The document exists to stop assets going
live, and a note phrased as an option gets treated as one.

### What is reproduced and what is added

**Every row is ACA's, in ACA's wording and ACA's order**, across all nine sections and the image-ad
page. The two-column marking is theirs.

**This skill's organisation, not ACA's:** rendering the sections as tables, the emitted CSV shape,
and the summary counts.

**This skill's additions, drawn from the document rather than stated by it:**

- ⛔ **The `Cannot assess` and `N/A` marks.** ACA gives two columns, Good to Go and Needs attention,
  and **no way to record that a row could not be judged.** With only two options, an unverifiable row
  gets ticked, and a filled checklist then certifies an asset nobody actually checked. This is the
  most important addition here.
- ⛔ **Naming which sections collapse without their inputs**: Creative Integrity needs the storyboard,
  Brand Compliance needs the guidelines, Hook Variation needs the rest of the batch, and the CTA
  alignment row needs the conversion objective. The document assumes an internal reviewer who has all
  four to hand.
- ⛔ **The rule that a failed Technical row means `hold` regardless of the performance half.** ACA
  orders the two halves and never says one gates the other.
- ⭐ **The observation that the six Hook Effectiveness rows are the five elements of a hook plus the
  pattern interrupt**, so failing three of them is an opener problem rather than six separate notes.
- ⭐ **The point that the heartbeat row is the most checkable in the document**, because a change
  every 1.5 seconds is countable from a timeline rather than judged.
- ⭐ **Reading the Final Gut Check as the part that cannot be gamed by a spec sheet**, and noting that
  ACA wrote *"(Be honest)"* into the document, which says what usually happens to it.
- ⛔ **Flagging the Branded / Native / Expert row as the only one naming a failure mode** rather than
  a quality, and the one with real consequences for a static.
- ⚠️ **The note that the captions row is two tests in one**, accuracy and placement.
- ⚠️ **Marking format-conditional rows `N/A` with a reason** rather than passing them by default.
- ⚠️ **The instruction not to soften a Needs attention into a suggestion**, since the document exists
  to stop assets going live.
- **The reminder to look at statics on a phone**, which is ACA's own instruction and is easy to read
  past.
