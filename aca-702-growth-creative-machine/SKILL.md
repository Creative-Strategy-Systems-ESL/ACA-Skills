## The through-line

> "**Creative excellence can come from practice and process and iteration, not just talent or
> inspiration.**"

⭐ **That is the whole course**, and it is the premise every other 700-Level course assumes without
arguing. This one argues it.

## How to work through it

**This is an argument, not a method.** Four steps, and it runs out honestly rather than being padded.

1. **Tell the parable** from `reference/the-machine.md`. It does the persuading, and ⭐ the point to draw out
   is that the quality group failed by *planning* — which is what a team making four polished ads a month
   believes it is doing right.
2. **Demonstrate the unpredictability** with the three ads in the same file. Let them guess before revealing
   which scaled.
3. **Give volume, velocity and versions**, and the decision tree for multiplying a winner's winning element.
4. ⛔ **Bound the numbers.** ⚠️ `reference/provenance.md` before quoting the 50-100 figure or the shelf-life
   stat: neither has conditions attached, and the real volume number comes from Course 703 or 601.
5. **Check they can say why the quantity group won.** If the answer is "they worked harder", the parable was
   heard as a work-ethic story rather than a process one.

## Answering well

**The parable does the persuading.** A photography class split in two: one side graded on quantity,
one on a single perfect photo. **The quantity group produced the best photos** — because they were
constantly shooting, experimenting and learning, while the quality group *"spent most of their time
overthinking and planning for perfection."*

**Nobody can pick the winner, including experts.** ⭐ *"I worked at Meta for nine years. I've seen so
many ads, concepts, campaigns, and I can tell you **even the best creative minds struggle to predict
a winner.**"* He then demonstrates it with three ads and the outcome is counterintuitive — **and the
winner still needed multiple iterations before it stuck.**

**Three parts: volume, velocity, versions.** Volume because shelf life is short; velocity because
tools collapsed idea-to-launch to hours; versions because a winner is a starting point, not a result.

⚠️ **His volume number comes with no conditions.** *"Between 50 and 100 new ads per week, including
iterations"*, attributed to *"the fastest growing performance advertisers"* — no spend level, no team
size, no category. ⛔ **Do not hand that number to an account without Course 703's five variables.**
