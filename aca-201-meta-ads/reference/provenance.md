## ⭐ Where module 8 was found, and why it matters

**Module 8, "Andromeda & Creative Diversity" (14m 37s), is not in the Course 201 transcript.**
Neither the delivered recording nor the production script contains the word "Andromeda" anywhere.

It was recovered from the **Course 202** export, where it sits at the back under the heading "Meta
Update on Andromeda Automation". Three things confirm it is Course 201 material:

1. Ashley opens it as an extra video giving a more current update on **Meta ads** in 2026
2. Its alternate intro says *"you've just watched the original **meta section** of this course"*
3. Its entire subject is Meta's delivery system, not creators

⚠️ **This is the second time an ACA module has been found bundled into a different course's export**
(Course 210's TikTok module sits inside a file named for Courses 702 and 703). Worth knowing if you
are matching this material against ACA's own files: a module missing from where it should be may be
at the back of a neighbouring one.

## ⚠️ The course contradicts itself, on purpose, and the skill preserves both sides

Module 4 teaches creative variation as colour swaps, background changes, logo moves and headline
tweaks. Module 8, recorded later, says that is no longer sufficient.

**Both are reproduced.** `targeting-and-creative.md` gives module 4 as taught and points forward;
`andromeda-update.md` gives the revision. Ashley flags the change herself — *"some of that used to
work better than it does now"* — and preserves a role for small variations once a concept is proven.

Flattening this into a single answer would lose the thing that makes it useful, which is that the
platform moved and she says so.

## What is quoted and what is organised

**Everything substantive is Ashley's, quoted directly.** **Quotes are verbatim.** This is spoken delivery, so they carry it as spoken — filler, false starts, mid-sentence restarts and the transcript's own slips. Nothing is smoothed, because a tidied quotation is no longer evidence of what was taught. The artefacts below are preserved where quoted and flagged, not corrected.

### ⚠️ Two transcription artefacts worth knowing about

**1. "Morella".** The delivered recording renders Mirella Crespi's name that way at the point where
Ashley points students at the five stages of market awareness. It refers to Mirella Crespi, ACA's
founder, and to her Course 102.

**2. "TripleLift" is almost certainly "Triple Whale".** The measurement section lists third-party
options as *"Google Analytics, TripleLift, or multi-touch attribution"*. TripleLift is an ad
exchange, not a measurement or attribution product, so it does not fit the sentence it is in.

The evidence that it is a mishearing: **every other place this class of tool is named across ACA's
material says Triple Whale** — including Ashley's own Course 202, which pairs it with Northbeam in
an ecommerce attribution context. Only this course says TripleLift.

**Left as recorded and flagged rather than silently corrected**, because the recording is the
artifact. But do not send anyone shopping for it: name the category (ecommerce attribution
platforms) rather than the garbled product.

**This skill's organisation, not ACA's:** the six-file split, the routing table, the "what dates
fastest" table in `watchouts.md`, and the decision to surface the module 4 / module 8 tension
explicitly rather than letting a reader hit it by accident. The module order in the course is the
order the reference files follow.

**The figures are ACA's** — 1.7 seconds of attention, ~70% of ROI traceable to creative quality, 4×
sales impact, 50% higher ROAS, 5% organic reach, Meta's 6+ placement recommendation, frequency of
2 to 3, ~4-week tests. Ashley cites them as study-derived without naming the studies, so they are
carried as her figures rather than as independently verified fact.

## What this course is not

Meta only. No TikTok, Google or other platform. No craft instruction on writing or designing an ad
— it covers what Meta rewards, not how to make it. No vertical benchmarks, no rate cards, no
click-by-click Ads Manager walkthrough.
