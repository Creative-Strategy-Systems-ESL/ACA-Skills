# The shared language of production

> "The most important part of effective collaboration between creative strategy and creative
> production is **a shared common language.** Remember those storyboard templates we covered? The
> sections for shot type and visual direction are where this shared language comes into play."

> "After producing **tens of thousands of paid social ads**, I've identified essential shot types
> that every native producer or content creator must master. **Think of this as your visual
> vocabulary.**"

---

## The visual vocabulary: four families

⚠️ **ACA's two recordings of this course list a different number of shots.** The written script gives
twelve; the delivered recording adds **Before & After** as a thirteenth. All thirteen are carried
here, and the discrepancy is flagged rather than resolved. See `reference/provenance.md`.

### Talking head shots

| Shot | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Selfie** | The creator filming themselves directly. ⭐ *"Ideally with some natural movements and fun angles, so it really feels authentic"* |
| **Showcase** | Talking while demonstrating the product |
| **Greenscreen** | Using platform background effects for context or emphasis. Shot on the effect, then layered onto a background |

### Product shots

| Shot | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Product in Hand** | Showing scale and real-world context. ⭐ *"Amazing for videos and static ads"* both |
| **Product Demo** | Demonstrating how it works |
| **Unboxing** | Capturing the opening experience. ⭐ *"Always try to shoot these with sound on for that extra ASMR effect"* |
| **Macro Texture** | Close-ups of materials, textures and important details |

### Action shots

| Shot | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Lifestyle** | The product or service being used in daily life |
| **Mirror Selfie** | ⭐ Showing before/after or full view. *"Amazing to show the before and after effect in full view"* |
| **Creator Demo** | Walking through the usage steps of the product, service or app |
| **Before & After** | ⚠️ *"actions that highlights the problem or the problem being solved."* **In the delivered recording only** |

### POV shots

| Shot | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Digital Interaction** | Point-of-view shots of browsing websites, apps, or playing a game |
| **Receiving Product** | *"Those first impression moments of picking up the package from the door and opening it from the POV angle"* |

### ⭐ Why the list is a bank, not a menu

> "Having these shots in your content bank allows you to **create endless variations of successful
> ads.** Master these, and you'll always have the building blocks you need."

> "When shooting content, **B-roll is your best friend.** Always try to capture as much B-roll as
> possible. Having a rich content bank you can pick from when creating ad variations gives you so
> much creative freedom and flexibility."

⭐ **The economic argument sits underneath this.** A shoot planned around one ad produces one ad; the
same shoot planned around the thirteen shot types produces a bank that feeds a quarter of testing.
The marginal cost of the extra shots, while the creator and the product are already in the room, is
close to zero.

## The filmmaker's toolkit

> "Even though we're often creating low-fi, native content that doesn't look like traditional ads,
> **we still need to think like expert filmmakers.**"

> "For a Creative Strategist to effectively communicate their vision, you need to understand basic
> cinematography techniques and language."

### Shot types

| Shot type | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Close-up** | Tight framing on specific details |
| **Wide Shot** | Capturing the full environment |
| **Extreme Close-up** | Dramatic detail focus |
| **Low Angle** | Filming from below — worm's eye view |
| **High Angle** | Filming from above — bird's eye view |
| **Over the Shoulder** | Viewing from behind a subject |
| **Canted Angle** | Tilted frame, for tension or energy |
| **Point of View (POV)** | Showing what the subject sees |
| **American Shot** | Framing from mid-thigh up |
| **Level Shot** | ⚠️ Looking at the subject directly. **In the delivered recording only** |

### Camera movements

| Movement | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Pan** | Moving side to side |
| **Tilt** | Moving up and down |
| **Track** | Moving alongside or around a subject |

> "Using this shared language ensures everyone on your team understands exactly what you're
> envisioning."

⭐ **Three movements is the whole list, and that is the point.** The vocabulary is deliberately small
enough to be learned in an afternoon by someone who is not a filmmaker, which is what makes it get
used in briefs rather than admired in a document.

⚠️ **ACA ships a guide of these definitions with visual examples and it is not in this folder.** The
recording says *"we created a super handy guide, available in your downloads below. It has all of the
definitions with visual examples of each shot."* The definitions above are complete as taught; the
visual examples are not held.

## The Video Techniques Database

> "Now I want to introduce you to **one of my most precious resources.** I honestly can't believe I'm
> giving this away. I have spent years carefully curating this."

**The argument for it:**

> "A key part of creating content that looks and feels native is understanding the visual language of
> each platform. Content creators on TikTok and Reels are constantly pushing boundaries with
> innovative transitions, effects, angles and edits. **There's so much we can learn from them.**"

**The seven techniques she names as examples of what is in it:**

| # | Technique |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Inside-the-box POV shots** |
| 2 | **The clone effect** |
| 3 | **Burning paper transitions** |
| 4 | **Ceiling-mounted camera angles** |
| 5 | **iPhone inception techniques** |
| 6 | **Screen-scrolling effects** |
| 7 | **On-screen writing and annotations** |

⛔ **The database itself is a Notion page and it is not in this folder.** These seven are the ones she
names aloud, and they are what is held. Do not present them as the database.

⭐ **Her instruction is to build your own anyway:** *"I highly recommend that you start gathering your
own references and build on this database."* The seven above are enough to start one, and a
self-built collection is the thing that stays current.

**Her pro tip, and the reason it belongs in the storyboard rather than a folder:**

> "Use these when you're building your storyboards and briefs. **You can add a link or a screenshot
> to the references column.** By applying these techniques, you can create truly engaging ads that
> speak the native language of the platform."

⚠️ **Platform technique lists date fastest of anything in this course.** Transitions and effects are
fashion; a technique that reads as native this quarter reads as dated the next. Check any of these
against what is actually running before briefing one.
