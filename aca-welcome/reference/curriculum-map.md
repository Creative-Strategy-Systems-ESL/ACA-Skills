# The Ad Creative Academy curriculum

The shipping curriculum. **26 numbered courses · 89 modules · 13 faculty · 17h 20m of video ·
10 levels (000 to 900).** This file is the authority on ACA course codes, faculty, companies,
runtimes and module lists.

The 26 are Courses **101 through 803**. Course 100 (the welcome and course outline) sits at the
000-level and is orientation rather than one of the 26, and the 900-level certification is an exam
and a capstone rather than a course. So a complete walk through the programme is 28 things: the
orientation, the 26 courses, and the certification.

⚠️ **On the 17h 20m: ACA's own summary says 34h 40m, which is a double-count.** Its spreadsheet's
runtime column carries a time on every course row and also on every level-summary row, and a level
row is just its own courses added up, so summing the column counts the same footage twice. Summing
the 26 course runtimes listed below gives 17h 20m 11s; the 8 level totals come to the identical
figure, and so do the 89 module runtimes. The per-course and per-module times below are ACA's own
and unaltered, so the arithmetic is checkable from this file.
