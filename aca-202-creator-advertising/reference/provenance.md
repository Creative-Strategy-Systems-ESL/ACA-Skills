## What is quoted and what is organised

**Everything substantive is Ashley's, quoted directly.** **Quotes are verbatim.** This is spoken delivery, so they carry it as spoken — filler, false starts, mid-sentence restarts and the transcript's own slips. Nothing is smoothed, because a tidied quotation is no longer evidence of what was taught. The artefacts below are preserved where quoted and flagged, not corrected.

⚠️ **Transcription artefacts in the source worth knowing about:** the delivered file renders several
phrases imperfectly, including "new writers" for *new riders* throughout the rideshare example,
"objective handling" for *objection handling* in the first statement of the eight jobs, and "greater
content" for *creator content*, and **"by cheap by twice"** for the proverb *buy cheap, buy twice*. All are preserved where quoted; none changes a framework.

**This skill's organisation:** the six-file split, the routing table, and the decision to give the
casting brief its own file rather than leaving it inside the casting lesson.

**Figures are ACA's.** The follower arithmetic in myth 1 — $10,000 on one 1M-follower creator at an
assumed 10% organic reach versus $2,000 plus $8,000 of media at an assumed $5 CPM — is Ashley's
illustration with her own stated assumptions, not a benchmark. `myths.md` says so and tells the
reader to rerun it on the client's real CPM.

## Scope, and where it hands off

Ashley points **three times** at **Chloe's course** — ACA's Course 502, UGC talent — for the
practical side of sourcing, briefing, casting, reviewing and managing creators:

> "This course is here to help you zoom out and understand the strategic role of creators and
> advertising."

So this skill deliberately does **not** carry: sourcing tactics, a full production brief template,
rate benchmarks, contract language, or creator management process. Where someone needs those, name
the gap rather than filling it.

She also declines to repeat her own Course 201 material on measurement, attribution and
test-and-learn, referring readers back to it instead. The measurement content here is only what is
specific to creator work.
